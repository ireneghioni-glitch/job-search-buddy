**The Research**
 Your PhD research will focus on
 **employee\-customer relationship management** 
 (acquisition, development and retention) within the
 **cleaning services sector** 
 , exploring how machine learning and AI agents can generate insights and improve processes.
   

  

 The exact research topic will be defined together with the PhD candidate and may include:
   

  

* AI\-driven employee and customer acquisition
* Consultant coaching effects on development and churn
* Skills, knowledge and behaviour (KSB) matching in recruitment and retention
* Employee absenteeism and performance modelling
* Pricing and price sensitivity analysis
* Hyper\-personalised employee and customer communication using genAI
* Process automation using genAI and reinforcement learning


 All research is conducted in close collaboration with the business and combines state\-of\-the\-art technology, real\-world data, advanced modelling, and academic and managerial reporting.
