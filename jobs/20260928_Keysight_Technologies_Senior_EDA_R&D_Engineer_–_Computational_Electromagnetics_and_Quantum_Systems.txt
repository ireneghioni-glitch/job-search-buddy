 We are seeking a
 **Senior R\&D Engineer** 
 to contribute to the development of the next generation of computational electromagnetic (CEM) solvers that power Keysight's simulation portfolio. This role focuses on the core numerical engine, including Finite Element Methods (FEM), Method of Moments (MoM), Finite Difference Time Domain (FDTD), sparse matrix solvers, meshing technologies, eigenvalue and eigenmode analysis, high\-performance parallel computing, and advanced boundary condition formulations.
   

  

 The ideal candidate will have a strong foundation in computational electromagnetics, numerical methods, scientific computing, and software engineering, with a passion for developing robust, scalable, and high\-performance simulation technology. You will work on the numerical infrastructure and algorithms that enable the efficient and accurate solution of Maxwell's equations for complex electromagnetic systems.
   

  

 A major focus of this role is enabling simulation technologies for
 **superconducting quantum processors, resonators, couplers, packaging structures, control and readout networks, and cryogenic microwave environments.**
 While the end applications are quantum\-focused, the work itself centers on advancing the
 **solver infrastructure, numerical algorithms, and software architecture** 
 that make these capabilities possible. You will work alongside experts in computational electromagnetics, quantum engineering, applied mathematics, and scientific software development to create scalable, production\-grade simulation technology used by researchers and engineers developing the next generation of quantum computing systems.
   

  

**Responsibilities**
 You will collaborate with multiple Keysight R\&D organizations to:
   

  

* Extend computational EM capabilities across Keysight simulation products
* Improve EM\-circuit\-system co\-simulation workflows
* Develop methodologies for advanced packaging, RF/microwave systems, and quantum technologies
* Create scalable simulation approaches that leverage modern HPC architectures
* Translate emerging customer requirements into reusable EDA capabilities


 Quantum computing is the main application area, but the primary objective is the advancement of computational EM technologies that support a broad range of engineering domains.

* Work with existing EDA product teams including computational EM, Quantum, RF/microwave, and circuit simulation groups to define and develop new simulation capabilities.
