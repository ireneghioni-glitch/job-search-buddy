Job Description:

NetImpact Strategies has an opportunity for a Curriculum/Instructional Designer to develop clear, accessible learning products that help federal users adopt enterprise applications and approved workflows as part of a multidisciplinary team supporting a federal customer. The mid-level specialist collaborates with operational and technical partners to deliver dependable services and measurable user outcomes; this position is remote.

**Responsibilities**
--------------------

* Design and maintain instructor-led, virtual, computer-based, microlearning, simulation, and job-aid content.
* Apply an approved instructional design method such as ADDIE to define learning objectives, content, and assessments.
* Create role-based learning paths and update content for releases, process changes, and recurring support issues.
* Develop materials suitable for approved offline or limited-connectivity use.
* Coordinate clinical content review and validation with credentialed clinical informatics personnel.
* Maintain content versioning, approvals, review status, and traceability to current workflows.
* Prepare content for approved learning systems and apply applicable accessibility requirements, including Section 508.

  
Qualifications:

**Required Qualifications**
---------------------------

* Bachelor’s degree in instructional design, education, learning technology, or related field; equivalent relevant experience considered.
* 3+ years of instructional design and curriculum development experience for adult learners.
* Proficiency developing technical training, assessments, job aids, and digital learning materials.
* Working knowledge of instructional design methods, accessibility, and content version control.

**Preferred Qualifications**
----------------------------

* Experience with SCORM, JKO, LMS administration, e-learning authoring tools, and Section 508 testing.
* Experience developing health IT, clinical application, or federal workforce training.
* ATD instructional design or comparable credential.

**Location Requirement**
------------------------

Remote (United States). Travel as required for approved program activities; frequency to be confirmed. Travel is subject to project needs and authorization.

Federal access requirements: Ability to satisfy applicable customer background screening, identity credentialing, and system-access requirements.
