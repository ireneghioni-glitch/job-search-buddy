AI Instructor – Live Online Group Classes (Teens & Adults)

**Company:** A Traveling Teacher Education LLC  
**Location:** Remote – United States  
**Job Type:** Part-Time, Independent Contractor (1099)  
**Compensation:** $60–$84 per teaching hour  
**Schedule:** Flexible, weekly one-hour sessions  
**Course Duration:** Four weeks per course

About A Traveling Teacher

A Traveling Teacher is an online education company dedicated to providing engaging, high-quality instruction through personalized learning and small-group classes.

We're expanding our educational offerings to include live Artificial Intelligence (AI) courses for both students and adults. We're seeking enthusiastic AI professionals, educators, and technology enthusiasts who can help participants develop practical skills and confidence using today's AI technologies.

**Our philosophy is simple: You teach. We handle the rest.**

We've built an automated system that manages student enrollment, scheduling, Zoom links, class organization, and post-class communications. Our instructors focus on delivering engaging lessons without the administrative responsibilities typically associated with teaching.

Position Overview

We are seeking knowledgeable and engaging AI instructors to lead live, interactive online group classes focused on understanding and effectively using artificial intelligence.

Our courses primarily utilize **Claude by Anthropic**, introducing participants to practical AI applications, effective prompting, and responsible AI use.

We offer separate courses for:

* **Middle and High School Students:** Age-appropriate AI education emphasizing foundational concepts, practical applications, critical thinking, and responsible use.
* **Adults:** Practical AI education focused on everyday productivity, professional applications, problem-solving, and effectively integrating AI into work and daily life.

**Instructors can choose to teach middle and high school students, adults, or both**, based on their experience, interests, and preferences.

Each course consists of four weekly, one-hour live sessions with a maximum of six participants per group.

All curriculum, lesson plans, and instructional materials are developed and provided by A Traveling Teacher. Instructors are not responsible for creating courses or developing curriculum.

Key Responsibilities

* Deliver engaging, live online AI lessons using our provided curriculum and instructional materials.
* Teach participants how to effectively use Claude and understand its practical applications.
* Explain AI concepts in clear, accessible language appropriate for the selected audience.
* Lead interactive demonstrations, discussions, and hands-on activities.
* Help participants develop effective prompting and AI problem-solving skills.
* Encourage critical thinking, responsible AI use, and an understanding of AI limitations.
* Answer participant questions during class and respond to occasional emails related to course content.
* Maintain a positive, supportive, and interactive virtual learning environment.
* Communicate relevant instructional concerns to our administrative team.

What You'll Teach

Our structured AI curriculum focuses on four core areas:

**1. AI Fundamentals**

* Understanding artificial intelligence and generative AI
* How large language models work
* Understanding AI capabilities and limitations
* Exploring practical applications of AI

**2. Prompt Engineering**

* Communicating effectively with Claude
* Writing clear and effective prompts
* Improving and refining AI-generated responses
* Using AI to approach complex questions and tasks

**3. Practical AI Applications**

* Hands-on experience using Claude
* Applying AI to research, brainstorming, and problem-solving
* Exploring AI for productivity, creativity, and learning
* Understanding how AI can support academic, personal, and professional goals

**4. AI Ethics and Responsible Use**

* Recognizing misinformation, bias, and inaccuracies
* Understanding privacy and responsible data sharing
* Academic and professional integrity
* Evaluating AI-generated information critically

Lessons and examples will be adapted to the appropriate audience, whether middle and high school students or adults.

Qualifications

We welcome applicants from a variety of educational and professional backgrounds.

* Professional experience working with artificial intelligence, particularly generative AI platforms.
* A background in education with strong knowledge of AI tools and applications.
* Experience teaching, training, mentoring, or presenting technical concepts.

* Excellent verbal communication and presentation skills.
* Ability to explain technical concepts in an engaging, accessible manner.

* Reliable internet connection and a professional remote teaching environment.

A teaching license is welcome but not required. We value practical AI expertise, communication skills, and the ability to make learning engaging.

Compensation

We offer a competitive compensation structure that rewards instructors as their classes grow.

* **Base rate:** $60 per teaching hour
* **Enrollment incentive:** An additional $4 per enrolled participant, per teaching hour
* **Maximum class size:** 6 participants
* **Maximum hourly rate:** $84 per hour
* **Course length:** Four instructional hours across four weeks

The enrollment incentive applies to each instructional hour and is not a one-time enrollment bonus.

Instructors are not responsible for recruiting participants. Compensation is based on enrollment for each session.

Additional course sections may become available as enrollment grows.
