**The Impact You’ll Make in this Role**  
The person hired for the position of Software Quality Engineer/Analyst will be part of the Clinical & Economic Research component development team. Testing of the component methodology will require assessing clinical specifications for procedures and diagnosis code-set changes, perform data analytics of healthcare claims to assess expected results with actual results covering complex clinical logic paths.  
  
As a Software Quality Engineer you will have the opportunity to tap into your curiosity and collaborate with some of the most innovative and diverse people around the world. Here, you will make an impact by:  
Analyzing and working with large sets of data (e.g. Healthcare claims)  
Development of test cases/test data needed to meet specifications, clinical content definitions and software implementation design/logic  
Execution of test cases (both manual and automated), analyze testcase execution to identify abnormalities and report defects  
Design/development of automation framework, scripts, execute automated scripts and analyze results  
Participate in scrum team planning sessions to identify dependency chain and tasks breakdown, provide estimates and outline test approach  
  
**Your Skills and Expertise**  
To set you up for success in this role from day one, Solventum requires (at a minimum) the following qualifications:

  

* Bachelor’s Degree or higher (completed and verified prior to start) from an accredited institution AND two (2) years of experience.

OR

  

* High School Diploma/GED or higher AND six (6) years of experience in the software industry.

AND

In addition to the above requirements, the following are also required:

  

* Three (3) years of combined experience creating software quality tests and/or data processing tests for non-gui applications.
* Experience testing applications that are implemented using JAVA, SQL, JSON Technology.

**Additional qualifications that could help you succeed even further in this role include:**

  

* Experience analyzing and working with large sets of data (e.g. Healthcare claims, Insurance claims, Financial transactions, etc.
* Experience working with an “Agile” team using Scrum Framework.
* Three (3) years experience testing applications or java jars/libraries with no user interface using JSON and CSV datasets.
* Experience with healthcare related data (claims and billing, healthcare informatics, inpatient and outpatient data) and HIPAA requirements is desired.
* Experience using Python/Java automation frameworks.
* Experience developing and executing test scripts using Java, Python, and SQL.
* Experience creating test preparation documentation and collaborating with cross-functional teams.
* Experience documenting test results, defects, and validation outcomes in accordance with established testing procedures.
