**Custom Truck One Source** (CTOS) is a leading provider of specialty trucks, heavy equipment, rentals, parts, service, and equipment solutions across North America, serving the utility, telecommunications, rail, infrastructure, forestry, and specialty contractor markets.

The Region Sales Support Specialist role performs duties related to the profitable sales and customer retention while projecting a positive image of the company. This role directly supports the company’s field sales team to ensure we deliver on-time and on-budget products that meets the customer’s needs in a profitable manner. This role supports the Region Account Manager team and reports to the Region Sales Manager.

**ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS**

* Assign incoming leads to the appropriate reps/regions
* Review salesperson backlogs for accuracy. Keep reps up to date on completions, delays and or changes
* Work with reps to facilitate and populate monthly forecast in SalesForce.com. You will be responsible to report this to the RSM
* Facilitate pre paints with appropriate build locations and customer visits. Also responsible to keep the timelines accurate throughout the build and escalate when barriers are encountered.
* Support Acct Manager in reviewing custom build quotes for accuracy prior to submitting to customer
* Work with accounting to ensure revenue recognition is completed and accurate
* Coordinate with logistics to dispatch sold trucks to customers. Utilize freight software to assist in the movement of vehicles and customer pick-ups.
* Work with title to ensure docs are sent out in a timely manner. Also request title mock ups, temp tags and any additional title paperwork needed to complete the sale.
* Align with M3 coordinators ensuring all orders are completely placed and accurate.
* Assist in gathering customer information to facilitate invoicing and title completion
* Support the Sales team in quote creation and modification to meet client RFQ requirements.
* Support the Sales staff work in SalesForce.com and required company documentation.
* Perform additional duties as assigned.
* Candidate must reside in Charlotte, NC and/or surrounding areas
