Summary:

The Epic Reimbursement Contract Specialist is responsible for building and maintaining the HB (Hospital Billing) and PB (Professional Billing) expected reimbursement contracts in Epic while acting as a subject matter expert in variance reporting and reimbursement analysis. This position will work incoming flagged underpayments and overpayments, collaboratively appeal confirmed underpayment instances, and continuously build and maintain system contract configuration to ensure accuracy against payer reimbursement terms. Further, this role will perform extensive reimbursement analysis, pinpointing recurring themes surrounding underpayments and identifying patterns for underpayments based on payer, service line, and other factors.

**Minimum Qualifications:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Information Systems, Informatics, Business Administration or related field; equivalent combination of education and experience may be considered.
* Epic certification in Hospital Billing and Professional Billing Expected Reimbursement Contracts Administration or ability to obtain within six (6) months of hire.
* Three (3) years of direct experience working on Epic Contracts Reimbursements Hospital Billing/Professional Billing including but not limited to ability to translate payer contracts into Epic system specifications using contract build tools, understanding of medical terminology, claims billing, and both inpatient and outpatient coding, ability to utilize Epic reporting tools including Alexandria Report, Contract Variance Reports, and SlicerDicer to perform in-depth underpayment analysis and identify underpayment patterns and payer trends
* May consider five (5) years of experience in applicable clinical/business experience preferably in a multi-entity health care system, large hospital, physical office, or managed care organization.

**Essential Job Functions:**

In addition to the essential functions of the job listed below, employees must maintain timely completion of all required education, trainings, and certifications as assigned and in accordance with Bozeman Health policies, department-specific requirements, applicable certification program and registry requirements, and other applicable regulatory requirements.

* Design, build, analyze, test, and provide support for Epic’s Reimbursement Contracts
* Serve as the subject matter expert for Epic’s HB & PB Reimbursement Contracts modules
* Provide support to other revenue cycle areas regarding questions on calculations and reimbursement generated by the contract builds
* Liaise with IT and operations on Epic functionality, workflows, documentation, best practices, and change management
* Perform regular system audits to determine contract accuracy in HB & PB expected reimbursements
* Identify and reconcile all payment variances discovered during various audits conducted at Bozeman Health
* Maintain a schedule of key update dates for expected reimbursement rules, such as Medicare OP quarterly updates, yearly Diagnostic Related Group (DRG) updates, and yearly increases for the Managed Care contracts
* Communicate with payers and maintain up-to-date HB & PB contract inventory and payer-plan master files
* Coordinate and actively participate with other associates and leaders on the team to expand and grow their knowledge of contract reimbursement models
* Other related duties and responsibilities as assigned.
