We are seeking a Content Writer who reflects our core values and is passionate about producing high-quality, trustworthy content that supports our clients’ growth. In this role, you will create blog posts, website pages, social media copy, and other marketing materials that strengthen brand awareness and engage target audiences. While we believe in using AI for research and gathering ideas, our content is 100% human written.

Success in this position requires a commitment to continuous education, a proactive ownership mindset, and a resourceful, solutions-driven approach to every project. You should be comfortable communicating clearly with team members, going above and beyond to understand client goals, and consistently delivering polished, accurate work. If you have a strong writing background and are eager to grow as part of a supportive, expert-driven digital marketing team, we’d love to connect.

**Role Description:**

* Conduct thorough, credible research on industry-related topics to produce content that reflects trustworthy expertise.
* Create engaging and accurate content for clients, including website pages, blog posts, social media content, and other marketing materials.
* Help optimize content for SEO using best practices, continual learning, and data-driven insights.
* Write in various styles and tones to match each client’s voice—including technical and industry-specific content.
* Enhance articles with research, reputable sources, and relevant external references.
* Proofread and edit both your own work to maintain a high quality standard.
* Communicate proactively with the team regarding deadlines, project needs, or questions to ensure above-and-beyond client service.
* Demonstrate a resourceful, problem-solving mindset when gathering information, overcoming content challenges, or adapting to shifting project needs.
* Participate in ongoing learning related to SEO, content strategy, and digital marketing as part of our continuous education culture.

**Qualifications/Experience:**

* Degree or working toward a degree in English, Journalism, Communications, Advertising, Marketing, or a related field.
* Proven writing skills with strong creative instincts and adaptability across formats.
* Exceptional grammar, proofreading, and editing abilities.
* Excellent organization skills, strong attention to detail, and the ability to manage multiple deadlines.
* Openness to feedback with the ability to take direction—and take proactive ownership of assigned work.
* Comfortable working in a fast-paced environment with variety in topics, writing styles, and industries.
* Experience with SEO and search marketing strategies is a plus.
* Prior marketing or copywriting experience is a plus.
* WordPress experience is a plus.
* B2B Writing experience a plus
