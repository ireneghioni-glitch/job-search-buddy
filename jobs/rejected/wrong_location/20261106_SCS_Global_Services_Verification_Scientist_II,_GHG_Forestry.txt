**Company Overview**  
SCS Global Services is a pioneer and leader in the field of sustainability standards and third-party certification, working across the economy in the natural resources, built environment, food and agriculture, consumer products, and climate sectors for over 40 years. As a Benefit Corporation, we are committed to socially and environmentally responsible business practices, and through the application of sound science, we are driving positive change.

**Job Overview**  
SCS Global services' Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Verification Program has been verifying carbon offsets since 2008 and to date has verified over 380 million tonnes of CO2e, providing GHG verification services to a wide array of industries including manufacturing, transportation, municipalities, and non-profit organizations. The GHG Verification Program draws upon SCS Global Services established expertise to serve the global carbon market. SCS Global Services has been voted Best Verification Company several times. Buyers of SCS Global-verified offsets include several Fortune 100 companies and SCS Global Services is a recognized thought leader in the carbon and climate change communities.

The Verification Scientist II, GHG Forestry is a lead auditor responsible for conducting assessments under various voluntary and compliance carbon offset standards. Project types include those in the Forestry and Land Use category (e.g. Improved Forest Management, Afforestation/Reforestation, REDD+, and mangroves) and landscape-level sustainability programs. Areas of responsibility include document review, quantification, growth and yield modeling, geospatial analysis, onsite forest reconnaissance and measurement activities, interviews with stakeholders, and report writing for offset carbon projects and programs worldwide.

**Essential Duties and Responsibilities**  
Auditing

* Assess management practices and project documentation of carbon projects and programs

Quality Management
