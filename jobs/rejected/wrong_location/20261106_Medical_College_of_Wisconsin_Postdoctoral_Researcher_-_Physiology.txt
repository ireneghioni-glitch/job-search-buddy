### **Summary**

As a Postdoctoral Researcher, and according to the National Institute of Health (NIH) and the National Science Foundation (NSF), you will be an individual who has received a doctoral degree (or equivalent) and is engaged in a temporary and defined period of mentored advanced research training to enhance the professional skills and research independence needed to pursue his or her chosen career path. In addition, according to the National Postdoc Association (NPA), Postdoctoral appointees can pursue basic, clinical or translational projects so long as their primary effort is devoted toward their own scholarship. Postdocs are essential to the scholarly mission of the mentor and host institution, and thus are expected to have the freedom to publish the results of their scholarship.

### **Primary Responsibilities**

* Plan, design and execute complex research studies, procedures and protocols investigating the role of the brain renin-angiotensin system in blood pressure regulation and autonomic control.
* Perform technically advanced techniques including radiotelemetry, nerve recordings, spectral analyses of cardiovascular data, stereotactic microinjections, and molecular work with transgenic mice and vectors.
* Participate in discovery projects.
* Coordinate research study activities; lead and manage projects.
* Oversee organization, synthesis and analysis of data and findings.
* Prepare scientific reports, outcome findings and scientific manuscripts.
* Participate in meetings with principal investigator and research staff in which you will evaluate/interpret the validity of data, develop methodologies, and design and evaluate lab procedures.
* Maintain supply inventory, may require negotiation with vendors, and track purchase orders.
* May oversee the work of laboratory personnel including training and development as well as daily work direction, delegation and establishing priorities.
* Other duties as assigned.

### **Knowledge – Skills – Abilities**

* Required knowledge in biology, chemistry, mathematics, and statistics
* Documentation, records management and data utilization skills
* Complex problem solving, critical thinking, and resource management
* Scientific writing skills

### **Qualifications**

**Minimum Required Education:** PhD in a scientific field or MD

**Minimum Required Experience:** 0 years

**Preferred Education:** PhD or MD/PhD in physiology, neurosciences,

**Preferred Experience:** Extensive experience working with mouse models. Strong understanding of cardiovascular physiology, pathology, and pharmacology, as well as fundamental principles of neuroscience. Experience with methods used to assess cardiovascular and autonomic nervous system function. Experience with molecular biology techniques and bioinformatic approaches is desirable
