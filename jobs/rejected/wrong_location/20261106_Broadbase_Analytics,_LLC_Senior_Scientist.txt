Overview

Broadbase Analytics LLC is seeking a Senior Scientist to provide comprehensive technical and programmatic support to the Advanced Research Projects Agency for Health (ARPA-H) and its Program Managers. ARPA-H supports high-impact biomedical and health-related research and development intended to address important health challenges through ambitious, innovative, and potentially transformative approaches.

The successful candidate will work with ARPA-H Program Managers and senior leadership to assess emerging science and technologies, refine R&D strategies, support program execution and oversight, and inform decisions involving complex biomedical, health, and interdisciplinary research programs.

The Senior Scientist will evaluate complex research concepts, identify technical opportunities and risks, assess potential mitigation strategies, and translate scientific findings into actionable recommendations for government clients. The position requires the ability to work across scientific and engineering disciplines and connect advances in areas such as biology, medicine, biotechnology, bioengineering, chemistry, materials science, data science, and related fields to mission-driven R&D objectives.

Responsibilities

* Provide scientific and technical support to ARPA-H Program Managers and senior leadership.
* Synthesize complex scientific and technical information into clear assessments, recommendations, briefings, and other decision-support products.
* Support the development, refinement, and evaluation of biomedical, health, and interdisciplinary R&D program concepts, strategies, research plans, and technical objectives.
* Assess proposed research approaches, technologies, performers, and R&D efforts.
* Evaluate technical feasibility, scientific significance, potential impact, progress, milestones, deliverables, and alignment with program objectives.
* Identify technical risks, uncertainties, dependencies, and implementation challenges.
* Recommend practical strategies to mitigate technical and programmatic risks.
* Prepare, review, and edit technical documents and other program materials.
* Support technical reviews and engagements with researchers, academic institutions, industry, and other R&D organizations.
* Monitor emerging developments in science and technology.
* Identify opportunities across scientific and technical disciplines, including biology, medicine, biotechnology, bioengineering, chemistry, materials science, computational science, artificial intelligence, and data analytics.
* Prepare and deliver technical briefings to ARPA-H stakeholders and senior decision-makers.

Required Qualifications

* Ph.D. in a relevant scientific, biomedical, health, engineering, or interdisciplinary field, such as biology, biochemistry, chemistry, biomedical engineering, bioengineering, materials science, computational biology, data science, neuroscience, or a related discipline.
* Significant experience conducting, evaluating, managing, or advising on biomedical, health, scientific, engineering, or other mission-driven R&D programs.
* Experience developing or assessing R&D concepts, technical strategies, research plans, scientific priorities, or investment recommendations.
* Demonstrated ability to evaluate complex technical concepts and identify technical risks and uncertainties.
* Ability to develop practical technical risk mitigation strategies and exercise sound scientific judgment.
* Excellent technical writing, presentation, and communication skills.
* Ability to synthesize complex information and communicate findings clearly to technical and non-technical audiences.
* Ability to work effectively across multiple scientific and engineering disciplines, including areas outside the candidate’s primary field of expertise.
* Ability to work independently, manage competing priorities, and operate effectively in a fast-paced environment.

Preferred Qualifications

* xperience supporting a Federal research organization such as ARPA-H, DARPA, IARPA, ARPA-E, HSARPA, ONR, AFRL, R&E, or similar organizations
* Experience evaluating research proposals, scientific performers, technology development efforts, research portfolios, or interdisciplinary R&D programs.
* Experience translating emerging scientific or technological developments into R&D opportunities, program concepts, or investment strategies.
* Experience supporting senior government, executive-level, or scientific leadership stakeholders.
* Familiarity with scientific literature analysis, technology forecasting, bibliometric analysis, landscape analysis, or related methods for assessing emerging research and technology.

The position will support sensitive federal R&D activities for ARPA-H and may require eligibility for a U.S. Government security clearance.

Candidates must be eligible to work in the United States and comply with applicable federal security and contracting requirements.
