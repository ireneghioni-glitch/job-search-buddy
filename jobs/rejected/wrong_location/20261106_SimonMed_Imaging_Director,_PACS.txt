SimonMed is one of the nation's largest outpatient medical imaging providers, delivering advanced diagnostic imaging services across a growing network of centers nationwide. Our commitment to innovation, operational excellence, and exceptional patient care is supported by industry-leading technology, enterprise imaging platforms, and a culture focused on continuous improvement.

  

As we continue to expand nationally, we are seeking a visionary **Director, PACS** to lead the strategy, performance, and evolution of our enterprise imaging ecosystem across approximately 180 outpatient imaging centers.

  

**Director, PACS**

  

Lead the future of enterprise imaging by developing and executing the strategy, governance, and operational excellence behind one of the nation's largest outpatient imaging networks.

At SimonMed, the Director, PACS is responsible for providing strategic leadership and oversight for the organization's enterprise imaging environment, including PACS, Vendor Neutral Archive (VNA), enterprise imaging platforms, image exchange technologies, and related imaging systems. This leader will partner with clinical, operational, physician, and executive stakeholders to ensure imaging technology supports exceptional patient care, business growth, innovation, and operational excellence.

  

This role combines strategic vision with technical expertise and operational leadership. The ideal candidate will drive enterprise imaging transformation, lead large-scale technology initiatives, and ensure the performance, scalability, security, and reliability of critical imaging systems that support millions of imaging studies annually.

  

**What You Bring**

  

**Enterprise imaging leadership:** You develop and execute long-term enterprise imaging strategies that support organizational growth, operational efficiency, clinical excellence, and technology innovation.

  

**PACS and imaging informatics expertise:** You possess deep knowledge of PACS, VNA, enterprise imaging, DICOM, HL7, image exchange solutions, and healthcare interoperability standards.

  

**Technology transformation experience:** You successfully lead complex implementations, upgrades, migrations, acquisitions, integrations, and enterprise-wide imaging initiatives.

  

**Operational excellence mindset:** You use data, metrics, and best practices to optimize system performance, improve workflows, enhance user experience, and support scalable operations.

  

**Strategic partnership management:** You build strong relationships with vendors, physicians, operational leaders, and executive stakeholders while ensuring service excellence and accountability.

  

**Innovation focus:** You evaluate and implement emerging technologies, automation solutions, AI-enabled tools, and new imaging capabilities that advance organizational objectives.

  

**Cross-functional leadership:** You collaborate effectively across IT, clinical operations, radiology leadership, physicians, cybersecurity, compliance, and executive teams to achieve enterprise goals.

  

**What You'll Gain**

  

**Enterprise-wide impact:** Your work directly influences imaging operations, physician productivity, patient experience, and technology performance across approximately 180 imaging centers nationwide.

  

**Strategic leadership:** Shape the future of the organization's imaging technology roadmap and enterprise imaging strategy.

  

**Technology innovation:** Evaluate and implement advanced imaging technologies, AI-enabled applications, automation tools, and next-generation imaging solutions.

  

**Operational influence:** Lead enterprise initiatives that improve scalability, reliability, workflow efficiency, data governance, and service delivery.

  

**Executive collaboration:** Partner closely with senior leadership to align imaging technology investments with long-term business and clinical priorities.

  

**Organizational growth:** Play a critical role in supporting acquisitions, de novo center openings, technology integrations, and strategic expansion initiatives.
