BiOrigin Specialty Products produces sustainable specialty paper and tissue products across five North American mills. We use a scientific, data-driven approach to deliver high-performance solutions, from antimicrobial coatings to water-based food service barriers. We are building a strategic sourcing organization to leverage our mill network, strengthen supplier partnerships, and create value for stakeholders.

**Position:** Senior Product Development Manager – Specialty Paper and Functional Coatings

  

**Reports to:** Senior Vice President of Strategy & Development

**Location:** Remote – travel up to 70%

The Senior Technical Advisor, New Product Development – MG Paper will lead the technical and commercial development of new machine-glazed paper products through BiOrigin’s five-step stage-gate process. This role will evaluate market opportunities, define product and manufacturing requirements, develop cost budgets and business cases, and support scale-up and launch to deliver profitable revenue growth and long-term value creation across MG paper, barrier, waxing, lamination base, food service, and flexible packaging applications.

**Role and Responsibilities:**

* Shepherd all new MG paper product development initiatives through a disciplined five-step stage-gate process from concept and scoping through business case development, product development, validation, scale-up, launch, and post-launch performance review.
* Lead cross-functional alignment across commercial, operations, mill technical teams, finance, sourcing, quality, and supply chain to evaluate, prioritize, and execute new MG paper product opportunities.
* Develop and maintain market analysis for new MG paper product concepts, including customer needs, competitive landscape, addressable market, pricing assumptions, channel fit, and strategic alignment with BiOrigin’s mill capabilities.
* Build and manage development cost budgets for trials, raw materials, coatings, waxing, converting, testing, packaging, qualification, customer validation, and commercialization activities.
* Partner with finance and commercial leadership to prepare business cases, including Net Present Value (NPV), revenue, margin, capital requirements, working capital, risk assumptions, and expected profit contribution following scale-up and launch.
* Define technical product requirements and translate customer critical-to-quality (CTQ) needs into MG paper specifications, functional coating formulations, application methods and operating parameters, performance targets, trial plans, quality standards, and manufacturing readiness criteria.
* Clearly define customer critical-to-quality specifications for each new MG paper product and validate that product performance, process capability, and quality requirements are met during customer scale-up trials.
* Coordinate mill trials, laboratory testing, customer qualification, product validation, and corrective actions to ensure new MG paper products meet appearance, barrier, containment, printability, converting, cost, quality, regulatory, and customer requirements.
* Provide senior technical guidance during product scale-up, commercialization, and early production to reduce execution risk, improve manufacturability, and accelerate time to market.
* Track post-launch performance against approved business case assumptions, including revenue, margin, cost, quality, customer acceptance, and operational performance, and recommend actions to improve value realization.
* Create clear gate review documentation, decision criteria, risk assessments, timelines, project charters, and executive summaries to support leadership approval and resource allocation.

**Desired skills and experience:**

* Bachelor’s degree in paper science, chemical engineering, mechanical engineering, chemistry, business, or a related technical field; advanced degree preferred.
* 10+ years of experience in MG paper, specialty paper, food service packaging, flexible packaging, coatings, waxing, converting, or related paper manufacturing environments, with demonstrated new product development leadership.
* Preference for hands-on experience with functional coating technologies, including barrier and oil- and grease-resistant (OGR) coatings, with demonstrated capability in coating formulation, raw-material selection, application methods, process optimization, performance testing, and translating customer critical-to-quality (CTQ) requirements into scalable product and manufacturing specifications. Strong understanding of MG paper attributes, fiber furnish, wet-end chemistry, surface properties, machine glazing, basis weight, caliper, strength, printability, converting, packaging, quality systems, and customer qualification requirements is also required.
* Proven ability to lead stage-gate or phase-gate new product development processes with disciplined project governance, technical validation, and executive decision support.
* Experience developing business cases that include market sizing, pricing, cost modeling, capital assumptions, NPV, revenue forecasts, margin expectations, and post-launch profitability tracking.
* Strong financial acumen with the ability to translate technical product decisions into commercial impact, manufacturing cost, customer value, and long-term profitability.
* Demonstrated ability to influence cross-functional teams without direct authority and communicate effectively with executives, mill leadership, commercial teams, customers, suppliers, and technical personnel.
* Highly organized, analytical, and results oriented, with strong project management, problem-solving, risk management, and continuous improvement skills.
* Willingness to travel to mills, suppliers, customers, and trial locations as required to support product development, scale-up, and launch execution.
