Lone Wolf simplifies real estate for the people who make it happen, and how we tell that story matters everywhere it shows up. We’re looking for a versatile multimedia designer who can bring our brand to life across every medium, from a printed one-pager to a 30-second animated video, and make it all feel like one consistent brand.

  

### **Responsibilties:**

* Creates engaging, on-brand visual and interactive content
* Combines graphics, animation, video, audio, and text
* Works across every medium and format Lone Wolf uses to reach customers, prospects, and internal teams
* Is a generalist creative role: one week might mean cutting a customer testimonial video, and the next might mean building a trade show banner, an email template, or an animated social ad

### **Requirements::**

* 3 to 5 years of experience in multimedia, graphic, or motion design, with a portfolio showing range across multiple mediums (digital, print, video)
* Proficiency with Adobe Creative Suite (Premiere Pro, After Effects, Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign) or equivalent tools
* Experience producing both digital and video content and print-ready design
* Proficiency with AI tools and a commitment to continuous learning and adoption of emerging technologies
* Strong eye for visual design, typography, and brand consistency across formats
* Comfortable moving between fast-turn digital work and longer production cycles (print, video)
* Experience with audio editing and sound design
* Presentation design (PowerPoint) experience
* B2B, SaaS, or real estate marketing experience a plus
