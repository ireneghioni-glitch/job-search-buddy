#### **Job Info**

Lead distribution channel growth & manage strategic accounts

Our client, a leading provider of specialty chemicals, is seeking an experienced commercial leader to drive revenue growth through its global distribution network. This role will own channel strategy, including developing new distributor partnerships, managing existing relationships, and maximizing channel performance. The position will also manage strategic global accounts and build relationships with executive-level customers.

Excellent opportunity for an experienced channel manager to combine commercial strategy, business development, and high-level relationship management.

#### **Key Responsibilities**

* Develop global channel strategies to drive revenue growth
* Manage existing distributor relationships while identifying and onboarding new channel partners
* Maximize distributor performance by providing training and monitoring sales activity (pipeline, forecasts, and KPI tracking)
* Lead negotiations and pricing contracts with distributors
* Manage executive-level relationships with key global customers, with responsibility for account plans, commercial negotiations and contracts
* Manage and develop an inside sales team member

#### **Qualifications**

* Bachelor’s degree in Chemistry or related scientific discipline
* 7+ years of commercial experience in life sciences or specialty chemicals
* Demonstrated success developing distributor networks, managing channel partnerships, and growing strategic accounts
* Proven track record of revenue growth and new business development
* Executive level relationship-building and negotiation skills
* Strong analytical skills, including advanced Excel proficiency for pricing analysis, forecasting, and sales reporting

Competitive base salary plus bonus and excellent benefits.
