We are a software company looking for a skilled, organized, and detail-oriented PHP & Database Programmer to help develop, maintain, and improve our websites, Web-based applications, and internal systems. This position involves hands-on programming, including writing, testing, debugging, and maintaining code; developing new features; and troubleshooting technical issues. The ideal candidate has strong PHP development skills, enjoys solving technical problems, and can work independently while collaborating with other developers and departments.

Our company develops and sells high-end professional design software – specifically, add-on programs for AutoCAD, SketchUp, and Revit – to landscape architects, irrigation designers, architects, and engineers. We started as a father-son duo working out of a garage and have grown into a company with more than 40 employees, providing best-in-class software to thousands of professionals in dozens of countries.

Land F/X is a fully remote company, with employees working from home across multiple states. We’ve embraced remote work while continuing to develop excellent software, provide exceptional customer support, and maintain a collaborative team environment.

* Develop, maintain, and improve the Company’s websites, Web-based applications, and internal systems using PHP, JavaScript, CSS, jQuery, MySQL, and other applicable technologies.
* Write, test, debug, and maintain code in accordance with established Company standards and development practices.
* Develop and maintain Web-based CAD dialog boxes and related functionality.
* Develop and maintain the Company’s internal administrative systems (IAS), Customer Portal, CRM, and data entry systems.
* Troubleshoot programming errors, database issues, and website or application performance problems.

* Follow established coding standards, security requirements, source control procedures, and testing practices.
* Research new technologies and development tools, including AI-based tools, and recommend improvements as appropriate.
* Maintain technical documentation and appropriately document code changes and development work.
* Assist with technical needs related to trade shows, conferences, marketing initiatives, and Company events as assigned, including occasional on-site work.
* Perform other programming, website development, and related duties as assigned based on Company needs.
