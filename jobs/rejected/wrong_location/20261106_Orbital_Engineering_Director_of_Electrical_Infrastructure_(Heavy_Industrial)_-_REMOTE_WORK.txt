**Job Description:**  
The Director of Electrical Infrastructure serves as the practice leader for the Electrical Infrastructure Practice within Orbital Engineering's Heavy Industrial Services Division. This position is responsible for the strategic growth, technical excellence, operational performance, and profitability of the practice and its associated service offerings.
  
  
The Director provides leadership across the full lifecycle of electrical infrastructure services, including risk-based asset assessments, asset management and long-term capital planning, electrical reliability and resiliency programs, arc flash and power system studies, detailed engineering and design, and the integration of maintenance and testing services through Orbital's affiliate organizations. Working across the industrial markets of manufacturing, power generation, metals, refining, petrochemical, and specialty chemical, this role leads the team that delivers solutions that improve the safety, reliability, performance, and longevity of critical client electrical assets.
  
  
This is a REMOTE WORK position that will interface with teams and resources across the United States and international locations.
  
  
Essential Functions
  
  

* Lead and grow the Electrical Infrastructure Practice within the Heavy Industrial Services Division.
* Develop and execute practice growth strategies, business plans, and revenue objectives.
* Establish and maintain technical standards, methodologies, tools, and best practices.
* Serve as the senior technical authority for electrical infrastructure assessments, planning, engineering, and reliability initiatives.
* Develop strategic relationships with key clients and industry partners.
* Support strategic account management efforts and major business development pursuits.
* Lead proposal strategy, technical approach development, and major project pursuits.
* Identify emerging technologies, market trends, and service opportunities to expand practice capabilities.
* Oversee technical quality and project execution across all electrical infrastructure engagements.
* Collaborate with affiliate organizations to deliver integrated assessment, engineering, testing, maintenance, and reliability solutions.
* Recruit, mentor, and develop engineering and technical professionals within the practice.
* Establish key performance indicators for safety, quality, client satisfaction, utilization, growth, and profitability.
* Serve as a visible industry leader through conference presentations, publications, client workshops, and thought leadership initiatives.
