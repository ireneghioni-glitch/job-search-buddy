**Your Role:**

EMD Electronics is seeking a Senior Scientist in Analytical Development with expertise in NMR spectroscopy.

In this role, responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

* Method development for the analysis of organometallic compounds and their impurities by NMR and support method transfer to quality control.
* Maintain the R&D dedicated 500 Mhz NMR and its subsidiary components.
* Analysis of samples and associated data to support R&D timelines.
* Act as a technical expert supporting EMD R&D staff with advanced NMR analyses.
* Act as the subject matter expert on NMR for EMD customer facing meetings when requested.
* Generate SOPs and technical documents/reports as necessary.
* Maintain an R&D notebook in accordance with ISO9001 standards.
* Support site NMR improvement initiatives and cross site harmonization.

* Be a technical resource for supporting quality control units associated with EMD product lines.
* Perform other (non-NMR) analytical method development and testing as required by the business.

**Who You Are**

**Minimum Qualifications:**

* Masters’ Degree in Chemistry, Materials Science or other life science discipline and 3+ years’ experience with hands-on experience with NMR.

OR

* Ph.D. in Chemistry or Materials Science Materials Science or other life science discipline.

AND

* 1+ years’ experience in routine maintenance of NMR equipment.

**Preferred Qualifications**:

* Experience interpreting NMR spectra of organometallic compounds.
* Experience with Topspin and MNova software.
* Experience with multivariate analysis and statistics.
* 2+ years’ experience performing routine maintenance of NMR equipment.
* Experience with NMR of transition metal nuclei.
* Experience with NMR experiments (2D, Cryogenic, Nonstandard Nuclei).
* Experience with Topspin and MNova software.
* Familiarity with advanced NMR experiments (2D, Cryogenic, Nonstandard Nuclei).
