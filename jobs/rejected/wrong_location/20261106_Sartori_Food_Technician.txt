SUMMARY

The Food Technician provides hands-on technical support to the Research and Development (R&D) team (TAG+APP) to advance cheese and product innovation initiatives. The role prepares and executes laboratory, pilot-plant, converting, and industrial trials; coordinates samples and testing; operates and maintains equipment and inventories; and accurately records and summarizes results. By ensuring safe, organized, and reliable trial execution and high-quality data, the Food Technician helps the R&D team make informed decisions, improve products and processes, and deliver on project and business priorities. This position will work primarily from 8am to 4pm, Monday - Friday, and will need flexibility in starting/ending times due to business needs.

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES include the following. Other duties may be assigned as needed.

* Prepares, sets up, and assists in the execution of R&D cheese make and converting trials, as well as analytical, pilot-plant, and industrial trials.

* Supports the preparation and fulfillment of internal and external sample requests for non-finished goods cheese items.

* Sets up, operates, disassembles, and cleans R&D pilot equipment; verifies equipment, materials, and trial readiness before execution.

* Maintains pilot-plant inventories of raw materials, ingredients, trial products, and related supplies, including organization, rotation, and disposition of expired materials.

* Performs physical and analytical testing using R&D laboratory equipment and application testing of product samples; submits samples for Quality Assurance laboratory testing as needed.

* Coordinates microbiological sample collection, identification, and submission for approved black-boxing or process studies in partnership with Quality Assurance or the microbiology laboratory.

* Records, organizes, and performs routine analysis of data from benchtop tests and pilot experiments; prepares data summaries and reports for review by the project lead.

* Assists with the manufacture of Sartori starter culture custom blends.

* Prepares samples for and participates in product evaluations and sensory tastings.

* Maintains accurate laboratory, trial, sample, and inventory records through data entry and other documentation systems.

* Participates in quality, product-improvement, and productivity initiatives.

* Maintains a clean, organized, and safe R&D work environment, including sanitation of the laboratory, coolers, pilot equipment, utensils, and work surfaces.

* Delivers against established R&D and Sartori company metrics and initiatives.

* Executes work using approved scientific protocols, food science principles, and project-tracking tools.

* Adheres to Good Manufacturing Practices, accurate documentation practices, safety requirements, and confidentiality expectations.
