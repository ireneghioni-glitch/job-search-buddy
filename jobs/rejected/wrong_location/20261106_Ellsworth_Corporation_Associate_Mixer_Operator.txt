**Responsibilities:**

  

* Prepares and loads non-hazardous raw materials into the mixer and sets up equipment to process materials per batch sheet instructions
* Ensures product quality by adhering to batch sheet instructions and collects sample of products for lab testing
* Accurately recording process data to batch sheet along with other production documentation as required
* Perform general maintenance and basic troubleshooting of equipment to maintain a safe work environment, quality product, and efficient production
* Prepare and package finished product per batch sheet instructions as needed
* Contributes to the maintenance of a clean and organized facility
