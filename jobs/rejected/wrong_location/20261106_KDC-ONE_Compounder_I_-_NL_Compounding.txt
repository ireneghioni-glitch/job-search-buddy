KDC is a network of best-in-class custom formulators and manufacturers of color cosmetics, skincare, haircare, bath & body, fragrance, deodorant, home, health, industrial, and auto care products. KDC delivers high-touch innovation, operational excellence, and speed to market to well-known and emerging beauty, personal, and specialty companies from coast to coast. KDC has its head office in Longueuil, Québec. In addition, KDC has 10 operating facilities located throughout North America. Altogether, the company employs nearly 4,800 talented and dedicated employees in North America.
  
  
Description / Scope / Mission
  
  
Manufacture a high quality intermediate and bulk product using process equipment available in a safe and cost-effective manner. This is an entry level position.
  
  
Key Responsibilities/ Accountabilities
  
1. Understand the operation, methods, and all techniques used in the manufacture of chemical products.
  
2. Sanitize and assist in preparations for product making.
  
3. Blend batches with assistance as needed.
  
4. Able to identify the correct raw materials for batches.
  
5. Pass forklift certification.
  
6. Wear provided safety equipment, i.e., dust mask, respirators, gloves, etc., when handling raw materials in areas subject to odors, fumes and high temperatures.
  
7. Follow established safety procedures and exercise good safety practices to prevent accidents to self, others and facilities.
  
8. Maintain the work area and equipment in a clean, orderly fashion.
  
9. Communicate promptly and openly to Compounding Leads all operational and personnel problems.
  
10. Execute special assignments as directed by the Compounding Leads.
  
11. Handle all chemicals in accordance with State and local regulations.
  
12. Maintain surveillance of your limited access area in the facility to be sure only authorized individuals enter the area.
  
13. Perform other duties as required and requested by Compounding Leads.
  
14. Able to read and understand SDS.
  
15. Can perform aseptic sampling of raw materials, intermediates and bulks.
  
16. Utilize a computer for data entry including batch information, downtime tracking and tank status.
  
17. Maintain accurate inventory by using scanner to document consumption of raw materials and components used during manufacturing.
