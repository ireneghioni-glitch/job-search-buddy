**The Opportunity**

This position can be either remote or work out of our St Paul, MN location, supporting our Structural Heart medical device division.

Our business purpose is to restore health and improve quality of life by designing and providing device and management solutions for treating structural heart disease.

As the TMVR Imaging Analysis Manager, you will lead the team that conducts imaging analysis in support of the patient selection and screening processes for clinical studies associated with Abbott Structural Heart Devices and Products, particularly Transcatheter Mitral Valve Replacement (TMVR). The incumbent will work closely within the internal clinical study team, including the clinical science, project management, field clinical affairs, medical affairs, biostatistics, study management, and data management, as well as with external investigators. This position will have significant interaction with executive leadership, physician investigators and clinical operations teams.

**What You’ll Work On**

  

* Patient screening and selection for first-in-class Transcatheter Mitral Valve Replacement (TMVR) clinical studies
* Developing the required patient specific analysis to enable patient selection for TMVR studies, based on complex review of medical imaging data, as well as other materials to support trial enrollments.
* Independently owning the image analysis process, end-to-end, from image acquisition to final patient suitability recommendations
* Proactively and critically examining ways to enhance overall clinical trial performance nationally and internationally.
* Providing ongoing feedback concerning all aspects of study screening experience for the purpose of developing and implementing strategies that will contribute to organizational and corporate goals and objectives.
* Collaborating with the internal core clinical study team and clinical sites to share best practices and develop capabilities to optimize patient screening and selection processes.
* Complying with U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) regulations, other regulatory requirements, Company policies, operating procedures, processes, and task assignments.
* Assisting with design, development, and monitoring of clinical evaluation projects.
* Mentoring and training image analysis team members to increase team productivity and achieve goals.
