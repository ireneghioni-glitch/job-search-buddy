 **Job Description**

The Associate Director, Clinical Scientist plays a key role in the execution of clinical development programs, partnering with Medical Directors and cross-functional study teams to support clinical data review, interpretation, study documentation, and communication of findings.

This is a hands-on role for an experienced Clinical Scientist who can work independently, identify and escalate meaningful clinical findings, and collaborate effectively across Clinical Operations, Data Management, Biostatistics, Pharmacovigilance, Regulatory, and other functions.

**Key Responsibilities:**

**Clinical Data Review & Study Execution**

* Lead ongoing clinical data review to support data quality, interpretation, and patient safety.
* Identify and escalate clinically relevant findings and partner with study teams to resolve data-related issues.
* Collaborate with Data Management and Clinical Operations on study implementation and EDC development.

**Clinical Documents & Data Outputs**

* Contribute to the development and review of clinical documents, including study protocols, investigator brochures, regulatory documents, concept materials, and other study-related documentation.
* Review and interpret clinical study data and outputs in partnership with Biostatistics, Pharmacovigilance, and other stakeholders.

**Cross-Functional & Scientific Leadership**

* Represent Clinical Development on cross-functional study teams and work closely with Medical Directors and other functional partners.
* Prepare and present clinical data, study designs, and scientific information for study teams, investigator meetings, advisory boards, study committees, and other internal or external audiences.

**Other duties and responsibilities as assigned**

 **Qualifications**

* PhD or equivalent degree with 2+ years of relevant experience, or bachelor’s/master’s degree with 5+ years of relevant post-graduate experience, including pharmaceutical industry experience in clinical drug or biologics development.
* Experience as a Clinical Scientist or in a comparable clinical development role with the ability to work independently.
* Hands-on experience reviewing, analyzing, and interpreting clinical trial data.
* Strong cross-functional collaboration, organizational, communication, and presentation skills.

Preferred Capabilities:

* Experience supporting Phase I-III clinical development programs.
* Clinical development experience in oncology and/or neurology.
* Rare disease experience.
