Requirements:

* Lead hands-on research projects that deliver business results.
* Work with operations to support new materials, quality concerns, and process improvements.
* Understand process capability and variability, design selection criteria, structure-property relationships, iterative problem solving, risk management, and intellectual property fundamentals.
* Perform as the Technical Lead on various project activities through a defined Stage Gate process.
* Work well within cross-functional teams to enable new product development project success.
* Work with site manufacturing and test engineers to complete product qualifications.
* Understand how to follow existing test methods (ASTM), and how to develop new test methods.
* Effectively communicate the right data to the right decision makes at the right time.
* Properly document technical and experimental activities.
