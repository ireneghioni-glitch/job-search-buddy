**The Opportunity:**

* An established global specialty materials manufacturer is seeking one experienced commercial professional to lead business development, sales and marketing across two complementary product lines: high molecular weight polyester resins for adhesives/coatings, and polyester elastomers including TPEE/COPE.
* Success requires meaningful technical fluency and an established customer network in relevant specialty chemical markets.

**Product and Market Focus:**

* High molecular weight polyester resins used in adhesives, paints and coatings, including coil and can coatings and hot-melt applications.
* Thermoplastic polyester elastomers (TPEE/COPE) used in automotive components, industrial goods, consumer products, footwear midsoles, breathable films, and wire and cable.
* Develop relationships with manufacturers, formulators, processors, OEMs and brand owners; identify high-value applications and adoption opportunities.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Own sales and strategic business development across both
* High Molecular Weight Polyester Resins for Adhesives and Coatings and
* Thermoplastic Polyester Elastomers (TPEE/COPE); prospect, qualify, cultivate and grow customer accounts.
* Leverage existing industry relationships to open doors with decision-makers, develop new applications and increase product adoption.
* Manage OEM and brand-owner initiatives from early technical discussion through testing, qualification and commercialization.
* Partner with technical, application development and R&amp;D teams on trials, customer support and troubleshooting.
* Monitor markets, pricing, customer needs, regulatory trends and competitor positioning; translate insights into actionable plans.
* Prepare customer presentations, technical/marketing materials, sales forecasts and account plans; maintain disciplined CRM records.
* Represent the business at customer visits, trade events and internal meetings as required.

**Qualifications:**

* Approximately 10 years of relevant sales, strategic marketing or technical business development experience in specialty chemicals or performance polymers preferred.
* Demonstrated commercial success in polyester resins, polyurethane/polyester adhesives, coatings, TPEE/COPE, TPU or closely related materials.
* Established relationships in adhesives/coatings and/or elastomer markets; ability to credibly develop both product portfolios is essential.
* Strong technical understanding of application development, customer testing and troubleshooting; manufacturing/supplier-side experience preferred.
* Experience engaging OEMs, brand owners, technical teams, purchasing and commercial stakeholders.
* Knowledge of relevant industry standards and regulations; ability to work across global, multicultural teams.
* Proficiency with CRM, forecasting and market/data analysis.

**Candidate Profile:**

* The strongest candidate is a commercially driven specialty chemicals professional who combines a portable network, technical product knowledge and a record of building revenue.
* Direct expertise in both portfolios is ideal; strong depth in one with credible adjacent experience and the ability to sell the other may warrant consideration.
