**Position Summary**

The HR Generalist provides hands-on and consultative support to employees and managers across functional teams, including but not limited to Engineering and Product Management, within the Couplings and Gearing Divisions. Partnering with Division HR leadership, HR Centers of Excellence, and HR Shared Services, this role primarily supports employee relations, talent processes, HR analytics, and HR operations.

  
  

**KEY RESPONSIBILITIES**

* **Employee Support & Relations:** Respond to inquiries and advise employees and managers on HR policies and processes; support performance improvement actions, investigations and conflict resolution; and identify effective solutions for workplace challenges under HR leadership guidance.
* **HR Programs:** Coordinate performance management, compensation planning, talent review preparation, open enrollment, and engagement surveys, including communications, data validation, completion tracking.

* **Recruiting:** Partner with Talent Acquisition and hiring managers on interviews, selection, and onboarding; coordinate internship, co-op, and early career program activities.

* **HR Operations:** Advise employees and managers on employee transitions, offboarding, leave of absences, accommodations, and payroll matters. Partner with HR Shared Services and specialist teams to resolve issues, guide return-to-work planning Support reporting requirements and ensure accurate employee records and pay changes, consistent policy application, compliance, and confidentiality.

* **HR Analytics and Insights:** Analyze headcount, turnover, hiring, engagement, leave, and other HR data to identify trends and emerging issues. Translate findings into actionable insights and recommendations for HR leadership, supporting workforce decisions and process improvements while ensuring data accuracy and confidentiality.

* **Engagement & Communications:** Support engagement strategies and action plans, workplace culture initiatives, organization updates, leadership announcements, and Division and HR meeting materials.
  

  

**Required Qualifications**

* Bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Organizational Development, or a related field.

* 2–5 years of HR experience, including hands-on generalist activities and/or HR operations.

* HR knowledge to include HR metrics, recruitment, employee relations, and HR compliance,

* Ability to exercise sound judgment handle sensitive and confidential information with discretion.

* Excellent listening, coaching, and verbal and written communication skills.

* Strong relationship-building and customer service skills. Credible, trusted, and influential with employees and managers at all levels.

* Demonstrated analytical and problem-solving skills, using data to inform decisions.

* Highly organized, including the ability to manage multiple priorities and projects simultaneously

* Proficiency in HR systems and Microsoft Office, including a strength with Excel.

* Ability to travel up to 10% within the region.
  

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Master’s degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or a related field.

* Experience supporting professional or corporate functional teams and geographically dispersed employees in global matrix organizations within manufacturing or industrial environments.

* Experience with Workday (HRIS) and Dayforce (payroll and Time & Attendance) Experience with payroll is a plus.

* Familiarity with AI tools and Power BI to support HR analytics and process improvements.

**About Regal Rexnord**
=======================

Regal Rexnord is a publicly held global industrial manufacturer with 30,000 associates around the world who help create a better tomorrow by providing sustainable solutions that power, transmit and control motion. The Company’s electric motors and air moving subsystems provide the power to create motion. A portfolio of highly engineered power transmission components and subsystems efficiently transmits motion to power industrial applications. The Company’s automation offering, comprised of controls, actuators, drives, and precision motors, controls motion in applications ranging from factory automation to precision control in surgical tools.

  

The Company’s end markets benefit from meaningful secular demand tailwinds, and include factory automation, food & beverage, aerospace, medical, data center, warehouse, alternative energy, residential and commercial buildings, general industrial, construction, metals and mining, and agriculture.

Regal Rexnord is comprised of three operating segments: Industrial Powertrain Solutions, Power Efficiency Solutions, and Automation & Motion Control. Regal Rexnord has offices and manufacturing, sales and service facilities worldwide. For more information, including a copy of our Sustainability Report, visit RegalRexnord.com.
