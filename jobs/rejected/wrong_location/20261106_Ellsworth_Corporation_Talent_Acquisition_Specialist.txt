**Responsibilities**

* Sources and hires talent for both exempt and non-exempt positions across the organization Reviews job applications, identifies potential candidates, screens candidates, schedules interviews, assesses talent, and recommends next steps in the hiring process
* Sources and engages candidates via external channels such as LinkedIn Recruiter, ZipRecruiter, Indeed, etc. crafting personalized outreach that highlights Ellsworth’s mission, cultural beliefs, and position
* Articulate Ellsworth’s value proposition to candidates, including mission, compensation, benefits, and work environment to influence talent to join the company.
* Builds rapport with hiring managers to understand the needs of each position through intake meetings to develop an effective talent acquisition strategy
* Promotes data integrity by keeping candidate notes and capturing status updates as well as interview documentation in the system to ensure legal compliance with OFCCP as well as corporate metrics
* Collaborate with senior manager to develop and implement pipeline strategies to proactively source applicants for posted and planned job openings
