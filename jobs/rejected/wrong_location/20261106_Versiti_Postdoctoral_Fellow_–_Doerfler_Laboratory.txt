Position Summary:

We are seeking a postdoctoral fellow to investigate the biology of human hematopoietic stem and progenitor cells (HSPCs), with a focus on how these cells respond to changes in their genome and maintain cellular function.

The research program examines the consequences of therapeutic genome editing in human HSPCs, including DNA damage responses, cell-cycle regulation, chromosome integrity, and changes in cellular state. Related projects investigate signaling pathways that regulate stem cell behavior and the persistence of cellular and epigenetic changes over time.

The position offers opportunities to pursue mechanistic questions using primary human CD34+ cells, genome editing approaches, hematopoietic model systems, and molecular and cellular assays. The fellow will lead research projects, develop experimental strategies, interpret results, and contribute to publications and collaborative studies.

**Research Environment**

The position is based at the Versiti Blood Research Institute. The fellow will work in a laboratory studying human blood stem cell biology and genetic therapies, with access to institutional expertise and shared resources in hematopoiesis, genome engineering, flow cytometry, genomics, and advanced microscopy.
