**What You’ll Do**  
As our Analytical Chemist, you’ll play a key role in releasing raw materials and finished products across our cosmetic, EPA, medical device, and OTC drug lines. You’ll work closely with Quality, Production, and other departments to keep products moving out the door on time and in compliance.

Your responsibilities will include:

**Analytical Testing**

* Test raw materials, bulk, and finished goods against approved specifications, methods, and USP/NF requirements.
* Perform titrations, pH, specific gravity, viscosity, and appearance testing.
* Run FTIR identification of incoming raw materials.
* Perform HPLC assay and identification of active ingredients and preservatives.
* Perform GC analysis, including alcohol content and residual solvents.
* Test the Purified Water System for TOC and conductivity (USP <643>/<645>).
* Test stability samples per protocol and pull schedule.
* Report OOS, OOT, and atypical results promptly and support lab investigations.

**Lab Operations & Instruments**

* Maintain, calibrate, and verify lab instruments, including HPLC, GC, FTIR, pH meters, titrators, and balances.
* Troubleshoot instruments, perform minor repairs, and coordinate vendor service.
* Prepare reagents, volumetric solutions, and mobile phases.
* Manage reference standards, lab supplies, and retain samples.

**Documentation & Compliance**

* Document all work accurately following Good Documentation Practices and ALCOA+ data integrity principles.
* Peer review analytical data as assigned.
* Follow 21 CFR Part 11 requirements for electronic records in chromatography data systems and LIMS.
* Follow cGMP requirements for OTC drugs (21 CFR 210/211), medical devices (21 CFR 820), and cosmetics.
* Follow the lab Chemical Hygiene Plan and all safety requirements, including chemical handling, PPE, compressed gas cylinders, and hazardous waste disposal.

**Continuous Improvement**

* Support development, transfer, verification, and validation of analytical methods per USP <1225>/<1226> and ICH Q2.
* Draft and revise test methods and lab SOPs.
* Recommend improvements to lab efficiency, methods, and compliance.

**What We’re Looking For**

* Bachelor of Science in Chemistry or a related scientific field.
* 2–4 years of hands-on experience in a GMP analytical/QC lab (pharmaceutical, OTC drug, cosmetic, or related industry).
* Independent operation and troubleshooting of HPLC and GC.
* Experience with titration, pH, and FTIR identification testing.
* Working knowledge of lab calculations and basic statistics (standard curves, %RSD, system suitability).
* Proficiency with Microsoft Office, chromatography data systems, and LIMS.
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
* Strong attention to detail, organization, and time management to meet release timelines.
* Method validation/verification and OOS investigation experience is a plus!
