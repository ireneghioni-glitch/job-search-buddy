**Program Analyst**  
Remote

Blu Omega is seeking a Program Analyst to support the supported mission area. This role is responsible for providing analytical and programmatic support to enhance organizational decision-making and operational efficiency within the Division of Extramural Programs at the National Library of Medicine, NIH.

**Program Overview**  
Supporting the federal mission to advance health and scientific research through data-driven program management and evaluation.

**Key Details**  
Location: Remote
  
Clearance/Suitability: Public Trust eligibility required

**Responsibilities**

* Collect, validate, analyze, and interpret administrative, financial, operational, grants management, scientific review, and program data from multiple sources.
* Develop reports, dashboards, visualizations, and briefing materials to support organizational planning and decision-making.
* Conduct trend analyses, forecasting, benchmarking, and performance assessments to identify opportunities for program improvement.
* Develop and track key performance indicators (KPIs) and evaluate program outcomes against organizational goals.
* Analyze organizational operations, workloads, and workflows, recommending process improvements to increase efficiency and effectiveness.
* Support strategic planning initiatives through comprehensive research and data analysis.
* Identify data inconsistencies and suggest improvements for data collection, reporting, and quality.
* Collaborate with leadership and cross-functional teams to identify analytical needs and communicate findings effectively.
* Support development of standardized reporting methods and performance metrics.

**Required Qualifications**

* Bachelor's degree in Data Analytics, Data Science, Statistics, Public Health, Health Informatics, or a related field.
* Experience supporting NIH programs, including familiarity with IMPAC II and QVR.
* Demonstrated experience in program analysis, data analysis, evaluation, and reporting.
* Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and experience creating dashboards, reports, and visualizations.
* Ability to interpret complex datasets and provide actionable insights.
* Strong analytical, organizational, problem-solving, and communication skills.
* Capable of managing multiple priorities and collaborating with leadership and stakeholders.

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Master's degree in Data Analytics, Public Health, Health Informatics, or related discipline.
* Previous experience supporting NIH, HHS, or federal health programs.
* Familiarity with NIH extramural research processes and grants management.
* Experience supporting strategic planning and operational assessments.
