**Position Description**
------------------------

The **Informatics Change Management Specialist** supports the planning, coordination, and execution of organizational change management (OCM) activities for clinical informatics, interoperability, and health information technology initiatives. This role partners with program teams, clinical stakeholders, and technical personnel to facilitate user adoption, stakeholder engagement, communication, training, and readiness activities that support successful implementation of new systems, workflows, and technology solutions.

The successful candidate will apply change management principles to help ensure smooth transitions and sustained adoption across healthcare environments while supporting Agile delivery teams and program objectives. Candidates must demonstrate hands-on experience working within Agile and SAFe delivery environments, supporting change activities aligned with program increments, release planning, and stakeholder readiness efforts.

**Responsibilities:**
---------------------

* Support development and execution of organizational change management strategies for clinical informatics and interoperability initiatives.
* Assist with stakeholder analysis, change impact assessments, and readiness evaluations to identify risks and adoption barriers.
* Develop and maintain change management artifacts, including communication plans, stakeholder engagement materials, readiness assessments, and adoption metrics.
* Coordinate communication activities to ensure stakeholders receive timely, accurate, and relevant project information.
* Support development and delivery of end-user training materials and job aids for clinical and technical audiences.
* Collaborate with Agile teams to align change management activities with project milestones, release schedules, and implementation plans, and SAFe Program Increment objectives.
* Participate in and support SAFe and Agile delivery activities, including Program Increment (PI) Planning, sprint reviews, backlog refinement, release readiness, and stakeholder engagement events.
* Participate in Agile ceremonies and planning activities to support stakeholder readiness and user adoption efforts.
* Monitor stakeholder feedback and adoption indicators, escalating concerns and recommending mitigation strategies when appropriate.
* Assist with implementation support activities, including go-live preparation, transition planning, and post-deployment adoption monitoring.
* Collaborate with VA facilities, program teams, and operational stakeholders to support successful deployment and sustainment of informatics solutions.
* Maintain project documentation and provide status updates related to change management activities.
* Support continuous improvement initiatives by gathering lessons learned and identifying opportunities to strengthen change adoption processes.
