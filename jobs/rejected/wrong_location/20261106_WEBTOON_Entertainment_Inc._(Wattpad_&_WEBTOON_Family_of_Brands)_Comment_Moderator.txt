We are looking for Comment Moderators to review and moderate user-generated content. As a Comment Moderator, you will review and investigate visual and written content that violates our Community Guidelines. You must be detail-oriented, culturally attuned, and self-motivated.

* This is a remote, part-time contract role.
* Bilingual fluency in English plus Hindi, Japanese, or Portuguese is preferred, but not required

### **Requirements**

* Prior hands-on experience moderating user-generated content (e.g. comments, forums, or social media trust & safety)
* Culturally aware of current trends, slang, and pop culture in the U.S. (a plus if also familiar with trends in Hindi-, Japanese-, or Portuguese-speaking markets)
* Technically savvy and able to quickly learn new systems
* High speed internet connection required
* Comfortable reviewing graphic, sensitive, or disturbing content as a routine part of the role
* Able to maintain strict confidentiality around user data and moderation decisions
