**Head of Chemistry (Medicinal and Computational)**

**Location:** San Francisco Bay Area (Hybrid / Remote-Flexible)

**Role Type:** Scientific Advisory (Phase 1) → Head of Chemistry (Phase 2)

**About the Venture**

We are a biotechnology startup based in the San Francisco Bay Area developing small molecules for treating aging-related diseases.

Our founding team brings deep expertise in molecular biology, translational medicine, and early-stage company building. We are seeking a **Head of Chemistry** to serve as our founding team member.

**Progression Structure**

We are structuring this engagement in two phases:

* **Phase 1 (Pre-Funding - Scientific Advisor):** Upon a successful mutual fit, we will onboard you immediately under an Advisor Agreement. In this phase, your commitment will be minimal (3-5 hours/month), focused on strategy reviews and high-level workflow alignment as we prepare for investor pitches. You will receive competitive equity for this role.
* **Phase 2 (Post-Funding - Head of Chemistry):** Upon closing of our funding round, transition into a full-time compensated role (Head of Chemistry) with competitive base salary and equity stake.

**Key Responsibilities**

**1. In Silico Design**

* Lead the Design-Make-Test-Analyze (DMTA) cycle, designing novel chemical matter with optimal potency, selectivity, and drug-like properties (ADMET/PK).
* Formulate clear Structure-Activity Relationships (SAR) and Structure-Property Relationships (SPR) using structure-based and ligand-based drug design (SBDD/LBDD).
* Implement advanced computational workflows to accelerate hit identification and lead optimization.

**2. CRO Operations**

* Architect and oversee an outsourced synthetic chemistry footprint across premier global CROs.
* Direct multi-step organic synthesis.
* Review raw analytical data (NMR, HRMS, LC-MS, ICP-MS) and troubleshoot synthetic bottlenecks.
* Establish rigorous quality control standards.

**3. Conjugate Architecture and Medicinal Chemistry Strategy**

* Direct the Design-Make-Test-Analyze (DMTA) cycle for novel heterobifunctional conjugates.
* Establish Structure-Activity Relationships (SAR) and Structure-Property Relationships (SPR).

**4. IP and Commercial Leadership**

* Partner with the CEO and patent counsel to evaluate and expand our Invention Disclosure Forms (IDFs), ensuring robust synthetic enablement, defensible Markush structures, and long-term Freedom to Operate (FTO).
* Act as the chemical design authority in investor meetings, partnering discussions, and Scientific Advisory Board (SAB) presentations.

**Required Qualifications (Must-Have)**

* **Education:** Ph.D. in Synthetic Organic Chemistry, Medicinal Chemistry, or a closely related discipline.
* **Domain Track Record:** 8+ years of biotech or pharmaceutical industry experience.
* **CRO Management:** Proven track record of architecting, contracting, and driving synthetic chemistry CROs through challenging multi-step organic syntheses, route scouting, and compound delivery.
* **Python proficiency:** Advanced Ability to build custom pipelines using Python.
* **Practical experience running Molecular Dynamics (MD)** simulations (e.g., GROMACS, Desmond, AMBER, OpenMM) to evaluate target-ligand binding kinetics, cryptic pockets, conformational flexibility, or other relevant applications.
* **IP Cleanliness:** Fully unencumbered by restrictive IP assignments, non-competes, or institutional conflicts of interest at the start of Phase 2 (paid role).

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Prior experience as a founder, consultant, or early scientific employee in a Seed- or Series A-stage biotech.
* IP cleanliness unencumbered starting in Phase 1 (Advisory).
* Experience working with heterobifunctional molecules.
* Experience with Density Functional Theory (DFT) or quantum mechanical calculations.
