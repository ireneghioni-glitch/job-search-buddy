**What you will do**

Let’s do this. Let’s change the world. In this vital role, the Executive Director, Digital Product Management - Research will be the senior digital technology leader for Research. The Executive Director will translate Research strategy and portfolio priorities into a coherent technology, data and AI agenda that accelerates science, improves the quality and reuse of scientific data, and creates durable advantage in how Amgen discovers medicines. As a member of the Research Leadership Team, the Executive Director will help transform how science is done, and shape investment priorities to ensure that digital technologies are actively moving the portfolio forward from disease understanding and target selection through molecule design and synthesis, and translational safety. Success would include evidence of better scientific decisions and faster experimental learning.The Executive Director will own the technology environment for Research, including benchtop environment, electronic lab notebooks, scientific workflows, and the data products and platforms that connect Research disciplines. The successful candidate will lead a talented organization and serve as a trusted thought partner to scientific leaders, ensuring that Research teams have dependable, well-designed capabilities that enable better and faster science.

  
  

**Responsibilities include:**

* Research strategy, portfolio impact, and executive leadership
* Set and continuously evolve the multi-year Research Technology, Data & AI strategy in partnership with Research, linking capability investments to the Research portfolio, scientific priorities, and measurable outcomes.
* Serve on the Research Leadership Team as a strategic advisor and decision-maker; bring technology and data insight to portfolio choices, operating plans, capital decisions, and risk discussions.
* Own portfolio prioritization and investment recommendations across the function, balancing scientific value, urgency, adoption, technical dependencies, risk, cost, and organizational capacity.
* Establish clear outcomes, OKRs and value measures; use evidence to redirect investment, retire low-value solutions and scale what demonstrably advances the science.
* Scientific technology, laboratory workflows, and data products
* Own the technology foundation that enables Research scientists to do their best work: benchtop and instrument-adjacent technology, electronic lab notebooks, LIMS, and integrated scientific workflows.
* Deliver reliable, scalable products that scientists want to use, improve workflows, and demonstrate measurable value.
* Lead the modernization and integration of scientific data workflows from capture at source through analysis, reuse, and decision-making, with a strong emphasis on data quality, metadata, terminology, and fit-for-purpose governance.
* Build a coherent portfolio of governed, reusable data products that connect experiments, observations, models, and knowledge across Research, making data discoverable, trustworthy, and ready for analytical reuse.
* Advance digital capabilities across the Research portfolio, including disease understanding, target selection, small molecule discovery, large molecule discovery, DNA-encoded libraries, and antibody-drug conjugates, while enabling productive collaboration across scientific domains.
* Ensure platforms and workflows are designed around scientist needs and continuously improve the end-to-end user experience.
* Technology, data, and operating leadership
* Lead and develop a high-performing organization spanning research informatics, laboratory and benchtop technology, digital products, and scientific data enablement.
* Build strong partnerships with Research scientific leaders, the Technology and AI & Data organizations, and external collaborators; resolve cross-functional dependencies and material trade-offs.
* Demonstrate robust technology investment discipline across platform rationalization, build-versus-buy decisions, and managing technical debt.
* Set a high bar for product and delivery excellence: clear problem framing, scientist co-design, strong architecture, rigorous vendor management, reliable operations, adoption, lifecycle management, and realized value
* Create a culture that combines scientific curiosity with accountability; one that moves quickly, learns from evidence, anticipates risk, and delivers dependable services to laboratories and research teams
* Communicate strategy, investment decisions, performance, risks, and opportunities clearly to executive audiences, translating complex scientific and technical choices into decisive action
