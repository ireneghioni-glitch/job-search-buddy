**BASIC FUNCTION:**
-------------------

  

This position will support the continued growth and development of the water and wastewater business in Northeast US region. The candidate will provide technical input and support, assist with market development and ultimately generate sales.

  

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**
---------------------

  

* Identify, evaluate and implement applications of water and wastewater products and solutions across the northeast US region.
* Understand and address both technical and commercial challenges.
* Develop the initial implementation through to the generation of revenue.
* Develop and implement Company technologies into potential industry applications.
* Responsibility for all phases of development from concept to trial and sale.
* Conduct and coordinate lab tests to develop products suitable for field trials and eventual commercialization.
* Provide on-site technical support during trials, pilot tests and implementation.
* Analyze and interpret data, making technical judgements to further projects.
* Propose and drive processing and technical solutions to industry challenges.
* Provide customer (internal and external) assistance and training.
* Generate detailed progress reports for management and peer review.
* Remain current on company and industry developments as well as technical understanding of target applications and industries.
* Demonstrate initiative in finding and pursuing potential customer leads, attending trade shows and conferences, initiating contact with various industry professionals to gain market knowledge and grow technical network.
* Perform other duties as assigned by supervisor.

Ideal candidate can be based in the Northeast or Mid Atlantic.

  

Technical – engineering skills for the application of products into the wastewater and water treatment process Marketing/Sales – understanding of wastewater business and market.
