**The Impact You’ll Make in this Role**

As our Saudi Arabia based **Advanced Healthcare****Implementation** **Engineer****,**you will have the opportunity to tap into your curiosity and collaborate with some of the most innovative and diverse people around the world. Here, you will make an impact by:

* **Interoperability & Integration Delivery**

Design and deliver secure, scalable integrations between Solventum solutions and customer healthcare systems. Work directly with customer IT teams, EMR vendors, and third-party providers to define, configure, validate, and troubleshoot interfaces using HL7, FHIR, APIs, and other interoperability standards.

* **Technical Discovery & Solution Design**

Conduct technical discovery activities to understand customer environments, infrastructure, workflows, and integration requirements. Translate business and

technical needs into practical implementation approaches that align with product capabilities and established delivery patterns.

* **Solution Configuration & Technical Enablement**

Configure Solventum solutions to support customer-specific operational and clinical requirements. Ensure implementations are delivered in accordance with best practices, while balancing customer expectations, technical constraints, and long-term maintainability.

* **Validation, Testing & Go-Live Readiness**

Lead technical validation activities to ensure configured solutions and integrations perform as intended. Support testing, defect investigation, issue resolution, and deployment readiness activities to enable successful customer go-lives and smooth operational transitions.

* **Advanced Technical Support & Continuous Improvemen****t**

Serve as an escalation point for technical issues during and after implementation. Partner with customers, support teams, product management, and other Solventum teams to diagnose problems, identify root causes, implement sustainable solutions, and drive continuous improvement across products and delivery practices. Capture implementation knowledge, best practices, and lessons learned, and share expertise with support, engineering, product management, and implementation teams to improve delivery quality, operational readiness, and customer outcomes across the organization.

**Your Skills and Expertise**

To set you up for success in this role from day one, Solventum requires (at a minimum) the following qualifications:

* Bachelor's degree in Health Informatics, Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience AND 5+ years of experience implementing, integrating, supporting, or delivering healthcare technology solutions, including experience working with healthcare interoperability standards, customer technical stakeholders, or healthcare information systems in a private, public, government or military environment
* Experience implementing enterprise software solutions in healthcare environments

  + Skills include:

  + Experience with EMR/EHR platforms such as Epic, Oracle Health (Cerner), Altera, Meditech, or equivalent systems.

  + Experience with interface engines, integration platforms, and familiarity with SaaS deployments, cloud-hosted solutions, networking fundamentals, or identity management concepts.

  + Experience supporting technical discovery, requirements analysis, solution configuration, testing, validation, go-live readiness, and production support activities.

  + Ability to perform root cause analysis, document implementation knowledge, and share lessons learned with support, engineering, product management, and implementation teams.

  + Knowledge of healthcare data privacy, security, and regulatory requirements.

  + Professional working proficiency in Arabic, including the ability to communicate with customer technical and operational stakeholders.

* Experience working directly with customer IT teams, technical stakeholders, and third-party vendors.

* Experience with healthcare interoperability standards, including HL7 v2.x, FHIR, APIs, and data exchange workflows.

* Experience troubleshooting complex technical issues across applications, integrations, and infrastructure components.

* Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain technical concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences.

* Ability to manage multiple customer engagements and priorities in a dynamic environment.
