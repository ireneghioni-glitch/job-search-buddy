**HVAC Install Helper**
=======================

**New Berlin Heating & Air Conditioning | New Berlin, WI**

**About New Berlin Heating & Air Conditioning**
-----------------------------------------------

New Berlin Heating & Air Conditioning helps homeowners stay comfortable through Wisconsin’s changing seasons. Our team installs and services a variety of heating and cooling systems, including furnaces, air conditioners, boilers, and indoor air quality equipment. We take pride in our work, support one another, and believe in helping people develop the skills to build successful careers in the trades.

### **Interested in learning a hands-on trade and building skills that can last a lifetime?**

We’re looking for a motivated and dependable **HVAC Install Helper** to join our team! This is a great opportunity for someone just starting out, whether you’ve recently graduated from high school or are looking for a career path that goes beyond the classroom.

No previous HVAC experience is required—we’re willing to teach the right person! You’ll work alongside experienced installers, learn how heating and cooling systems work, and gain valuable hands-on experience on residential job sites. If you enjoy working with your hands, learning new things, and being part of a team, this could be the perfect place to start your career.

**Benefits**
------------

* **Starting pay of $18 per hour**
* Hands-on training and mentorship from experienced HVAC professionals
* Opportunity to build valuable trade skills
* Career growth and advancement opportunities
* Supportive, team-oriented work environment
* Opportunity to develop a long-term career in the HVAC industry

**What You’ll Do**
------------------

* Help experienced installers set up and install heating and cooling equipment.
* Assist with carrying, loading, and organizing tools, equipment, and materials.
* Learn how to install ductwork and other HVAC components.
* Help prepare job sites and keep work areas clean, organized, and safe.
* Follow instructions and learn proper tool use and safety procedures.
* Work alongside teammates to complete jobs efficiently and correctly.
* Treat customers and coworkers with respect and professionalism.
* Ask questions, accept feedback, and build your skills through hands-on experience.
* Help maintain a clean and organized company vehicle.
* Take on additional tasks as you learn and grow in the role.

**What We’re Looking For**
--------------------------

* A positive attitude and willingness to learn.
* Dependability, punctuality, and a strong work ethic.
* Interest in learning a skilled trade and developing a long-term career.
* Ability to follow directions, ask questions, and work well with others.
* Good communication skills and a respectful, professional attitude.
* Attention to detail and a commitment to working safely.
* Ability to perform physical work, including standing, bending, reaching, and lifting equipment or materials weighing up to 70 pounds, with or without reasonable accommodation.
* Reliable transportation to job sites and a valid driver’s license, if required for the position.

**No HVAC experience, EPA certification, or personal tools are required to get started.** We’ll help you learn the basics and develop the skills you need to succeed!
