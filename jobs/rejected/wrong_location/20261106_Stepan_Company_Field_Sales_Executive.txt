**Job Description**
-------------------

Stepan is seeking a **Field Sales Executive** to join our Consumer Products Surfactant sales team in **North Texas**. In this role, you will report to the Regional Sales Manager and drive profitable growth across a portfolio of Direct and Distribution customers. You will also manage and expand existing business, build a strong pipeline of new opportunities, and coordinate cross-functional resources to deliver customer and business results.

  

**Candidates must reside within the North Texas region, including the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metroplex, San Antonio, Oklahoma, or the Memphis metropolitan area, and be able to effectively support customers and business activities throughout the territory.**

  

**Key Responsibilities**

* Manage and grow a portfolio of Tier 2 and Tier 3 accounts while identifying and pursuing new business opportunities within the assigned territory or market.
* Develop and execute territory and customer sales plans aligned with annual sales, volume, gross margin, and growth objectives.
* Build long-term relationships across customer purchasing, technical, commercial, and other decision-making stakeholders.
* Conduct customer visits and business reviews, advance opportunities through the sales process, and negotiate pricing and commercial terms within established guidelines.
* Partner with R&D, Technical Service, Marketing, Regulatory, Supply Chain, Customer Service, and Operations to address customer needs and coordinate trials, technical programs, and commercial follow-up.
* Maintain an active opportunity pipeline, accurate CRM/Salesforce activity, monthly forecasts, and contributions to annual sales planning and budgeting.
* Develop knowledge of target markets, technologies, industry trends, and competitive dynamics, and represent Stepan at customer meetings and relevant industry events.

**Qualifications**
------------------

* 1+ years of relevant sales or commercial experience required; 3+ years preferred, ideally in specialty chemicals or a related B2B industry.
* Demonstrated ability to build customer relationships, manage sales activities, and pursue new business opportunities.
* Foundational business and financial acumen, including an understanding of pricing, volume, margin, and profitability drivers.
* Strong written, verbal, presentation, relationship-building, and negotiation skills.
* Ability to collaborate effectively across technical and business functions.
* Strong problem-solving, organizational, analytical, and decision-making skills, with the motivation and initiative to work independently in a remote or field-based environment.
* Proficiency with CRM/Salesforce and standard business tools; Power BI, SAP, PowerPoint, Excel, and Word experience preferred. Ability and willingness to travel as required.

**Education**
-------------

* Bachelor's degree required; a technical or scientific discipline such as Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, or a related field is preferred.
