Responsibilities:

The Atmospheric, Climate, and Earth Sciences Division is seeking a **Computer Scientist** who:

* Designs and develops computers and computational systems, including both hardware and software, with a focus on architectures, languages, and applications.
* Researches and advances theories of computation, including the design and efficiency of algorithms and how humans interact with computers and computing processes.
* Develops work products including new theories and technical advances in the field.
* Lead software engineering and development efforts for the Energy Research and Forecasting (ERF) model, a GPU-enabled, compressible atmospheric flow solver built on the AMReX adaptive mesh refinement framework.
* Design, develop, and maintain performant, portable numerical algorithms for atmospheric dynamical cores, ensuring correctness and efficiency across CPU and GPU architectures.
* Lead GPU hardware portability efforts, optimizing ERF for heterogeneous DOE exascale and pre-exascale systems (e.g., NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel GPU platforms).
* Oversee deployment, build systems, continuous integration, and performance validation of AMReX-based models on DOE high-performance computing (HPC) systems (e.g., Frontier, Perlmutter, Aurora, and PNNL institutional computing resources).
* Collaborate with the multi-institution ERF development team to extend model capabilities, including terrain representation, urban simulation, land surface modeling, atmospheric chemistry, aerosols, and cloud microphysics.
* Mentor junior software engineers and postdoctoral researchers contributing to ERF and related AMReX-based codes; contribute to code review, documentation, and open-source software practices.
* Author and co-author technical publications, scientific publications, software releases and present ERF capabilities at DOE program reviews and scientific conferences.

Preferred Qualifications:

* PhD in Computational Science, Engineering, Applied Mathematics, Atmospheric Science, or closely related field, plus demonstrated experience in scientific software development.
* Demonstrated hands-on experience and leadership in developing production-quality scientific/engineering software in C++ for high-performance computing environments.
* Proven experience porting and optimizing scientific codes for GPU architectures (CUDA, HIP, SYCL, or portable abstraction layers).
* Experience deploying and validating large-scale simulation codes on DOE HPC systems.
* Experience with the AMReXframework or comparable block-structured adaptive mesh refinement (AMR) libraries.
* Experience developing, extending, or deploying the ERF model.
* Strong software engineering practices: version control, testing/CI, code review, and technical documentation.
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with a record of technical publications and/or open-source software contributions.
* Expertise in compressible dynamical core numerics for atmospheric or geophysical flow modeling.
* Track record of peer-reviewed publications on atmospheric models.
* Experience mentoring students/postdocs and contributing to multi-institution DOE software projects.

HSPD-12 PIV Credential Requirement:

As a national laboratory, PNNL is responsible for adhering to the Homeland Security Presidential Directive 12 (HSPD-12) and Department of Energy (DOE) Order 473.1A, which require new employees to obtain and maintain a HSPD-12 Personal Identify Verification (PIV) Credential. To obtain this credential, new employees must successfully complete the applicable tier of federal background investigation post hire and receive a favorable federal adjudication. The tier of federal background investigation will be determined by job duties and national security or public trust responsibilities associated with the job. All tiers of investigation include a declaration of illegal drug activities, including use, supply, possession, or manufacture within the last 1 to 7 years (depending on the applicable tier of investigation). Illegal drug activities include marijuana and cannabis derivatives, which are still considered illegal under federal law, regardless of state laws.

For foreign national candidates:

If you have not resided in the U.S. for three consecutive years, you are not eligible for the PIV credential and instead will need to obtain a favorable Local Site Specific Only (LSSO) Federal risk determination to maintain employment. Once you meet the three-year residency requirement thereafter, you will be required to obtain a PIV credential to maintain employment. The tier of federal background investigation required to obtain the PIV credential will be determined by job duties at the time you become eligible for the PIV credential.
