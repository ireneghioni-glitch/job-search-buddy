For our methionine production facilities in Antwerp, we are looking for a talented and ambitious Chemical Engineer who is interested in combining technical expertise with long-term career development opportunities within an international chemical company.
  
  
In this role, you will contribute to the safe, efficient and sustainable operation of our production plants. Your responsibilities will include:

* Monitoring and supporting daily production activities
* Assisting with production planning and process optimization
* Identifying and implementing process improvements
* Participating in investment and development projects
* Contributing to initiatives related to Process Engineering, Environment, Safety, Health & Quality (ESHQ), and Energy Management

### **Profile**

* Master of Science in Chemical Engineering or a PhD in Chemistry
* Strong interest in chemical manufacturing, process technology, ESHQ and energy efficiency
* Analytical mindset with a structured and solution-oriented approach
* Strong communication and interpersonal skills
* Proactive, responsible and able to work effectively in a team environment
* Ambition and potential to grow into positions with increased responsibility, including management opportunities within Evonik or at the Antwerp site
* Excellent command of Dutch and English; knowledge of German is considered an asset
