About the role

  

Our Technology Process Development team, composed of 7 people, is integrated in the Research and Innovation (R&I) department within the Peroxides Global Business Unit, and focuses on development, scale-up, industrialization, and deployment of new process technologies in collaboration with researchers, plant teams, project managers, R&I leadership and suppliers.

As a R&I Process Engineer who is passionate about research & innovation, you will contribute to the Global Business Unit's (GBU's) target in maintaining our leading edge technology and making process improvements through technical expertise and collaboration.

You will be accountable for :

* Develop process flow sheets, mass and energy balances, and simulation studies.
* Scale up laboratory process data and collaborate with manufacturing sites to identify the most integrated solutions.
* Analyze laboratory data, troubleshoot process issues, and recommend improvements.
* Evaluate techno-economic scenarios based on developed process flow sheets.
* Communicate project progress and present findings to stakeholders through oral presentations and written reports.
* Apply HSE rules and housekeeping practices while fostering a culture of safety.
* Contribute to laboratory safety and support the protection of people and assets.

We offer a fixed term contract (6 months, extendable) based on onsite working mode. The role is based in Brussels, with occasional travel to the Netherlands (approximately 1 to 2 days per month).

  

About you

Relevant Master's degree or PhD in Chemical Engineering. relevant work experience as a process engineer. Experience in the chemical industry is highly preferred.

  

Competencies:

* Strong knowledge of process design fundamentals, including PFDs, P&IDs and equipment sizing.
* Proficiency in Aspen Plus / Aspen HYSYS.
* Project management skills.
* English proficiency, additional languages are welcome.

Behaviors:

* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
* Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities.
* Collaborative mindset with an innovative approach to process development.
