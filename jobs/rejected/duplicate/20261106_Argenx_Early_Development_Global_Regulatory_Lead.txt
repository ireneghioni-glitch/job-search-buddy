For the expansion of our regulatory team, argenx is looking for an Early Development Global Regulatory Lead, EDGRL, to support the rapid growth of its early-stage pipeline.

  

**Key Accountabilities and Responsibilities:**
==============================================

  

The ED-GRL is a key member of the global Early Development regulatory team (EDRT). This role serves as a close business partner and facilitator of regulatory activities and is expected to contribute to developing sound regulatory strategies globally. The role plays a critical role across the Early Development organization, acting as a key strategic advisor and enabler of accelerated, patient-centric development pathways. In close collaboration with cross-functional partners and the broader regulatory team, this role provides high-quality, timely regulatory guidance to support early asset progression—from pre-nomination activities and scientific advice through early clinical development and regulatory interactions—ensuring robust regulatory foundations and smooth transition into later-stage development.

Your primary areas of focus are:

  

* Act as a key member of the EDRT establishing and growing the team in alignment with argenx culture
* Provide global regulatory leadership for assigned early development programs, ensuring clear regulatory strategy, risk identification, and alignment of cross-functional teams.
* Co-create and implement innovative global regulatory strategies to deliver expedited development milestones through end of first in human studies aligning with commercial objective
* Ensure patient-centricity is embedded in regulatory strategy
* Represent EDRT within key early development governance forums and ensure consistent regulatory input across stakeholders in a timely manner to support business decisions
* Serve as the conduit between EDRT and asset teams to facilitate a smooth transition of programs
* Partner with ED Regulatory Lead to develop and implement infrastructure and process improvements
* Mentor and coach EDRT members
* Build relationships with internal stakeholders
* Work with regulatory intelligence and policy teams to monitor, interpret and communicate evolving global regulatory requirements
* Oversee regulatory affairs vendors involved in EDRT activities and ensure high-quality interactions with regulators
Work with the rest of the argenx ED teams, provide hands-on contributions to regulatory filings including Scientific Advice, INDs, and other areas as requested  

**Desired Skills and Experience** :

  

* Minimum 15 years of regulatory affairs experience within biopharmaceutical organizations, of which at least 5-7 years must have been in global leadership roles with a focus on major regulatory regions such as FDA, EMA, or PMDA.

* Advanced scientific degree preferred, such as PhD or PharmD.

* Resonates with argenx values and culture, with a collaborative, humble and accountable approach to working across the organization.

* Strong understanding of drug development, with direct experience in early clinical development, first-in-human studies, orphan drugs and regulatory strategy for innovative medicines.

* Proven ability to develop fit-for-purpose global regulatory strategies that support early asset progression, regulatory interactions and informed development decisions.

* Regulatory experience across early and late clinical development phases, supporting continuity from discovery and first-in-human studies through later-stage development.

* Experience interacting with regulatory authorities, including scientific advice, IND/CTA activities, expedited pathways and other early development regulatory milestones.

* Strong scientific mindset, with the ability to engage credibly with scientists, clinicians and cross-functional development teams.

* Scientific depth in immunology is highly desirable, with broader experience across therapeutic areas, indications considered an advantage.

* Complementary expertise across modalities, including antibodies, peptides, siRNA, and small molecules.

* Experience integrating biomarker strategy, patient engagement and patient-relevant evidence into regulatory and development plans.

* Strong understanding of current and evolving global regulations, guidelines, and regulatory expectations and health care business practices relevant to early and late clinical development globally.

* Clear and influential communicator, with strong written, verbal and presentation skills and the ability to translate complex regulatory topics into practical business recommendations.

* Highly developed organizational and project management skills, with the ability to prioritize, delegate and manage multiple workstreams in a fast-paced environment.

* Strong matrix leadership skills, with the ability to build relationships, influence stakeholders, build, motivate and develop teams, and drive outcomes.

* Pragmatic, creative and hands-on, combining strategic thinking with the ability to execute and deliver high-quality work under challenging timelines.

* Additional experience supporting cross-functional business development due diligence activities and providing regulatory assessments to support investment and portfolio decisions is an advantage.

Interest in applying AI and digital tools to improve regulatory strategy, regulatory intelligence, document preparation and ways of working is an strong advantage.
