**Your role**
-------------

As a **QA Specialists supporting MES in Sterile Manufacturing and in Device Assembly and Parenteral Packaging** , you will use your understanding of sterile manufacturing processes to make informed quality decisions on the setup of the new PAS/X MES system. You will define quality requirements and review and approve relevant procedures and MES data to ensure alignment with GMP and applicable quality requirements. You will:

* Develop and maintain a thorough understanding of the processes within the Sterile Manufacturing area to make well-informed quality decisions in line with GMP and applicable quality requirements.
* Provide quality support for the MES project in Sterile Manufacturing area
* Review and approve quality-relevant master data, manufacturing instructions and process changes
* Participate in projects to optimise workflows for the review and approval of master data, manufacturing instructions and process changes
* Assure compliance with the marketing authorization and Good Manufacturing Practices
* Establish and maintain strong working relationships with the MES-team, Business and Quality partners to ensure alignment of objectives and results and act as Quality Point of Contact.
* Support the quality oversight process of the operational activities by ensuring QA review and approval of GMP documentation.
* Act as the quality representative for projects, providing quality oversight and ensuring alignment with GMP and applicable quality and compliance requirements.
* Provide general administrative support to the QA MES Lead

**Your profile**
----------------

* University degree, scientific orientation (pharmaceutical, chemical or biological sciences/engineering)
* 5-10 years experience in a Pharmaceutical Industry with knowledge of aseptic manufacturing, annex I and how this relates to set up of batch record and batch release activities
* You have a quality-driven mindset. You are familiar with the GMP legislation and deliver accurate and precise work.
* You have excellent computer proficiency with experience in MES
* You can demonstrate the ability to work independently while staying connected with key stakeholders.
* You can work well in a team and maintain an open and constructive dialogue with the various partners. You can deal with time pressure and changing priorities. You are able to make risk-based decision under time pressure.
* You strive to deliver results and progress. You have a pro-active attitude and are familiar with risk management principles.
* You are analytical and problem solving. You are constantly looking for continuous improvement
* You have a thorough knowledge of pharmaceutical legislation, in cGMP regulations (domestic & international), ICH guidelines, policies and procedures
* You are an excellent communicator, able to build a network and create win-win solutions
* You understand the business implications regarding quality positions and decisions
* Fluency in Dutch and English, both written and spoken
* You have a good mobility to ou client located in Beerse, Belgium
