Overview:

Bruker is enabling scientists and engineers to make breakthrough post-genomic discoveries and develop new applications that improve the quality of human life. Bruker’s high performance scientific instruments and high value analytical and diagnostic solutions enable scientists to explore life and materials at molecular, cellular, and microscopic levels. In close cooperation with our customers, Bruker is enabling innovation, improved productivity, and customer success in post-genomic life science molecular and cell biology research, in applied and biopharma applications, in microscopy, as well as in industrial and cleantech research, and semiconductor metrology in support of AI. Bruker offers differentiated, high-value life science and diagnostics systems and solutions in preclinical imaging, proteomics and multiomics, spatial and single-cell biology, structural and condensate biology, as well as in clinical microbiology and molecular diagnostics.

For more information, please visit www.bruker.com

The **Team Lead Assembly & Testfield (m/f/d)** is responsible for leading the Assembly and Test field teams involved in the mechanical and electrical assembly, system integration, software configuration, final testing, calibration, validation, and shipment preparation of Bruker MicroCT systems. The role ensures products are built, tested, and released according to quality, regulatory, and delivery requirements while driving operational excellence, continuous improvement, and employee development.

Responsibilities:

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Lead, coach, and develop the Assembly & Test teams, fostering a culture of accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement.
* Oversee the assembly, integration, testing, validation, and shipment readiness of Bruker MicroCT systems.
* Ensure production targets, quality standards, and delivery commitments are achieved.
* Drive troubleshooting, root cause analysis, and corrective actions to resolve technical and operational issues.
* Monitor performance metrics and implement process improvements to enhance efficiency, quality, and productivity.
* Collaborate with Engineering, Supply Chain, Quality, and Service teams to support production and operational goals.
* Support new product introductions, manufacturing readiness, and implementation of engineering changes.
* Ensure compliance with safety, quality, and regulatory requirements while maintaining a safe and organized work environment.

  
Qualifications:

* Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Electromechanics, Computer Science, Physics, or a related field.
* 7+ years of experience in high-tech instrumentation, system testing, manufacturing engineering, or a similar technical environment.
* Prior experience leading technical or operational teams preferred.
* Strong expertise in system integration, validation, and troubleshooting of hardware and software.
* Experience working with quality systems, regulated environments, and continuous improvement initiatives.
* Knowledge of X-ray imaging systems, analytical instrumentation, or Mass Spectrometry is an advantage.
* Fluent in Dutch and English, with strong communication skills.

### **Leadership Competencies**

* Proven ability to lead, coach, and develop high-performing teams.
* Strong focus on operational excellence, accountability, and results.
* Effective mentoring, problem-solving, and decision-making skills.
* Collaborative approach with the ability to build strong cross-functional relationships.
* Analytical, structured, and solution-oriented mindset.
