**About the team:**
-------------------

**Shape the future of renewable energy with us!**

  

ZF Wind Power is one of the world’s leading providers of wind turbine gearboxes and is currently being established as an independent entity within the ZF Group – with the clear goal of responding even more effectively to market and customer needs while enabling further growth. This step increases entrepreneurial flexibility and strengthens our role as a global technology leader in wind energy.

  

We have the world’s largest installed capacity of +8 MW offshore wind turbine gearboxes. Since entering the wind industry in 1979, we have delivered more than 85,000 gearboxes, powering over 200 GW wind turbines, covering more than 25% of the total installed capacity of geared-driven wind turbines worldwide. Together with our partners, we continuously invest in the wind market to empower a sustainable future together.

  

Because ZF Wind Power keeps on growing, we are looking for a **Senior Chemical Expert** to join our team in our headquarters in **Lommel.**

  

**What you can look forward to as Senior Chemical Expert Wind Power:**

* Responsible for gearbox oils (assessment and approval), sealants, process chemicals, chemical and surface cleanliness, corrosion protection, washing processes, paint & primer systems, packaging requirements, and chemical legislation
* Ownership and development of related specifications, standards, and qualification procedures
* Close collaboration with suppliers and customers, including technical assessment and qualification of chemical products
* Cross-functional work across departments (e.g. Technology, Process Engineering, Logistics, HSE) and countries (Belgium, Germany, India, China)
* Provide expert guidance on chemical selection, material compatibility, failure analysis, and process improvements while acting as the subject matter expert for chemical-related topics across the organization
  

**Your Profile as Senior Chemical Expert Wind Power:**

* Master’s Degree or PhD in Chemical Engineering, Chemistry, Materials Science & Engineering, or a related discipline (e.g. Polymer Science, Surface Engineering, or Corrosion Engineering)
* Strong knowledge of industrial lubricants and additives, tribology, polymers, coatings, corrosion protection, and chemical compatibility. Experience with wind turbine gearbox lubricants is preferred
* Experience with laboratory analytical techniques (e.g. FTIR, GC-MS) and chemical compliance frameworks and legislation, including REACH and RoHS as well as in developing technical specifications, standards, and qualification procedures for industrial products or processes
* Ability to provide technical leadership and expert guidance across multidisciplinary teams
* Strong communication skills, accountability, and a collaborative mindset with the ability to work effectively in global cross-functional teams
* Fluent in English; Dutch is an advantage and open for international travel
