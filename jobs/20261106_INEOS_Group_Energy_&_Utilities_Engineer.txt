**The Energy & Utilities Engineer helps o****perate and transform one of belgium's largest Industrial energy systems**

At our Jemeppe-sur-Sambre site, energy and utilities are critical enablers of industrial performance.

Our department is responsible for managing an energy ecosystem comparable to a small utility company, including:

* a 100 MW combined heat and power plant with two LM6000 gas turbines; a steam turbine and industrial boilers;
* electricity and natural gas procurement;
* participation in electricity markets and flexibility mechanisms;
* water abstraction, treatment and effluent management facilities;
* site-wide energy efficiency and decarbonization programs.

Together, these activities support more than 2 TWh of annual energy consumption and an energy spend of approximately €100 million per year.

As part of a small expert team, you will contribute to the reliable, competitive and sustainable supply of energy and utilities while helping shape the site's long-term energy strategy.

**What You Will Do**

You will work at the interface between operations, engineering, markets and sustainability.

**Energy & Utilities Operations**

Support the management and optimization of the site's energy and utility systems, including:

* 100 MW CHP plant; 2 x LM6000 gas turbines; steam turbine and 3 boilers;
* 60 MW Solar plant
* water supply and treatment facilities;
* effluent treatment installations.

You will collaborate closely with our operational teams to resolve issues, improve performance and support long-term reliability.

**Energy Performance & Continuous Improvement**

Using monitoring and analytics tools, you will:

* identify performance deviations and improvement opportunities;
* develop and implement energy-efficiency initiatives;
* support operating teams in achieving measurable results;
* transform operational data into actionable recommendations.

Our energy management program has already generated more than €2 million per year in recurring savings, with many additional opportunities currently under development.

**Projects & Decarbonization**

You will contribute to the identification, evaluation and implementation of projects such as:

* waste heat recovery;
* process electrification;
* renewable energy integration;
* steam system optimization;
* energy flexibility solutions.

You will help build the roadmap that supports the site's long-term decarbonization ambitions.

**Energy Markets & Commercial Optimization**

You will participate in:

* electricity and gas contract management;
* optimization of on-site generation assets;
* flexibility and ancillary services markets;
* forecasting and operational planning;
* evaluation of new market opportunities.

**Team & Stakeholder Support**

As part of a lean organization, you will also contribute to:

* technical coaching and knowledge transfer;
* contractor and service provider management;
* collaboration with production, maintenance and corporate teams.

**What We Are Looking For**

Master's degree in Engineering (Mechanical, Electrical, Electromechanical, Energy, Process Engineering or similar).

We are seeking an engineer who is excited by complex industrial systems and motivated by the opportunity to create tangible operational, financial and environmental impact.

Several years of experience in one or more of the following areas:

* industrial energy systems;
* utilities operations;
* CHP or power generation assets;
* energy management (ISO 50001, “Convention Carbone”);
* energy markets;
* industrial decarbonization;
* technical project management.

No candidate is expected to be an expert in every domain. What matters most is your ability to learn quickly, think analytically and take ownership of complex challenges.
