At Voxdale we're looking for a Product Designer to help us design products that matter and scale effortless.
  
  
The Role
  
  
You design products from brief to production-ready concept, grounded in clear value, shaped by real constraints. You translate the brief into designs that are meaningful, manufacturable, and worth building. You know your craft. You stay with the work until it ships.
  
  
What You'll Do
  
  

* Explore concepts — Generate and evaluate concepts that balance user value, technology, feasibility, and production method. Design for what can actually be made.
* Develop and validate — Carry concepts through to detailed design. Prototype regularly to validate ideas early, when changes are cheap.
* Build a convincing case — Present your thinking clearly to teammates across disciplines. Design is a dialogue, not a delivery.
* Contribute to your Tribe — Share knowledge, explore new methods, and bring what you learn back to the team. What you discover, everyone can use.

How We Work
  
  
Every project runs within a clear framework; someone connects strategy to daily work, and a shared system keeps the team aligned on priorities and ownership. We use AI tools where they help. You decide when and how, that's part of the freedom and responsibility we give everyone.
  
  
What Makes This Role Different
  
  

* You work on complex hardware for Life Science Medical, Industrial Robotics, Energy CleanTech, Food AgriTech, Consumer Electronics, Space Defense, and Science Instrumentation. Design decisions have real consequences.
* You're not handed a brief and left alone. You help shape the brief, then own the design response.
* Your work touches the full arc: from early concept through prototyping to manufacturing handover.

What You Bring
  
  
**Required:**  

* Familiarity with prototyping (physical and/or digital)
* Ability to design for manufacturability from day one (materials, production methods, constraints)
* The drive to see a product through to market, not just to a finished drawing or prototype
* Pragmatic and realistic, you know when good enough is right and when it isn't

**Nice to have:**  

* Experience with workshop facilitation or design research
* Circular design thinking

**Mindset:**  

* Holistic thinker who doesn't lose sight of the user while solving an engineering constraint
* Comfortable with ambiguity at the start of a project and disciplined enough to converge
* Eager to share knowledge and learn from others
* You'll be part of a close-knit team that stays in sync on what's happening and where things are headed.

Our Values in This Role
  
  

* Inventiveness: No copy-paste solutions. Every client brief deserves fresh thinking, not a template.
* Owning It: You guard design quality throughout the project.
* Fearless Feedback: Design critiques are how we get better. You give honest feedback and welcome it in return.
* Pioneering: You stay curious about new tools, methods, and materials, including AI, and bring what's useful back to the team.
