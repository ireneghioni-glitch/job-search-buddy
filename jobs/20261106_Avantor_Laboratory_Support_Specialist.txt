**The Opportunity:**
--------------------

Executes day-to-day lab operations & support objectives at customer facilities, following a defined standard output or set of procedures, focused on minimizing laboratory workflow interruptions. Problems and issues faced are generally recurring and solutions are typically known and/or prescribed. More complex problems are solved by applying methods learned through experience and/or using available support. Activities may include PPE and/or safety station checks and maintenance, reviewing defibrillators, extinguishers and dispensing areas to confirm working order, storage, tracking and distribution of chemicals/materials ensuring accurate inventory control, and hazardous waste collection and recycling. Collects, documents, and organizes data and observations. Communicates activity records, service level metrics and other information requiring some explanation or interpretation to team members, supervisors or customers.

We are looking for a proactive and organized **Laboratory Support Specialist** to support Sciences laboratory operations. The successful candidate will play a key role in ensuring laboratory equipment, , analytical systems, and scientific support activities are maintained to the highest standards, enabling scientists to focus on research activities.

  
  

This position is ideal for someone with a **background in Chemistry, Analytical Chemistry, Laboratory Sciences**, or equivalent practical laboratory experience.

  

**Key Responsibilities**
------------------------

### **Laboratory Equipment Support**

* Perform routine cleaning and maintenance of laboratory equipment.
* Clean refrigerator doors, seals, and perform freezer defrosting activities.
* Clean vacuum pump cabinets and cryostat/QDa filters.
* Replace activated charcoal filters and solvent waste vent filters.
* Clean rotary evaporator baths and ultrasonic baths.
* Clean analytical system OA components.
* Clean lyophilization equipment and associated filters.
* Clean NMR spinners and other specialized analytical equipment.

### **Technical Laboratory Support**

* Prepare solvents for UPLC/MS systems.
* Support purification activities through filling and emptying purification racks.
* Perform recovery testing on purification systems.
* Assist with preparation activities for qNMR analyses.
* Support routine laboratory technical operations.

### **Scientific Support Activities**

* Prepare QC plates for analytical testing.
* Inject standards and perform quality control runs on analytical systems.
* Ensure analytical systems are operational and ready for laboratory use.

### **Quality & Continuous Improvement**

* Participate in laboratory 5S activities and workplace organization projects.
* Maintain compliance with laboratory procedures and safety requirements.
* Identify opportunities to improve laboratory processes and efficiency.

  

**Candidate Profile**
---------------------

### **Required Education**

* Bachelor's degree in:
  + Chemistry
  + Analytical Chemistry
  + Biochemistry
  + Chemical Engineering
  + Laboratory Sciences

**OR**

* Equivalent experience in a pharmaceutical, research, analytical, or laboratory environment.

### **Required Experience**

* Experience working in chemistry, analytical, pharmaceutical, biotech, or research laboratories.
* Familiarity with laboratory equipment and scientific workflows.
* Experience handling laboratory consumables and stock management is an asset.
* Experience supporting chromatography systems (HPLC, UPLC, LC-MS) is highly desirable.

### **Technical Skills**

* Knowledge of laboratory good practices (GLP/GMP awareness is a plus).
* Understanding of analytical laboratory environments.
* Ability to follow SOPs and documented procedures.
* Comfortable with routine equipment maintenance and troubleshooting support.
* Good computer literacy (Microsoft Office).

### **Personal Skills**

* Strong attention to detail.
* Well organized and structured.
* Hands-on and service-oriented mindset.
* Reliable and accountable.
* Team player with strong communication skills.
* Ability to manage multiple priorities in a dynamic laboratory environment.

  
  

**Preferred Background**

We are particularly interested in candidates who:
