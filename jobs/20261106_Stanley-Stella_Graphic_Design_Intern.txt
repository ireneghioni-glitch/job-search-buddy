#### **Your purpose at Stanley/Stella**

As a **Graphic Design Intern**, you will join the Creative & Design team and support us on various projects linked to our B2B collections. You will contribute to the creation of visual materials for our collections and assist with day‑to‑day graphic tasks.  
This internship offers a hands‑on opportunity to discover the creative process within the textile industry while developing your technical and creative skills.  
**It is a 4 to 6 month internship that requires a university internship agreement.**

#### **Your role and impact**

Support the creation of full collection overviews:

  

* coloured up styles,
* layouts and organisation,
* printing and preparation of documents,
* attaching colour swatches
* (For both EU & US collections)
* Create simple graphic elements (illustrations, icons, small logos, placements)

  
Assist with daily graphic tasks, such as:  

* InDesign layouts, creative moodboards, presentations, trend boards and trend movies
* Contribute to brainstorming sessions and research for seasonal inspirations
* Conduct design and trend research (colours, graphics, techniques)
* Adapt designs to different formats and applications

#### **About you**

* Student in Graphic Design, Visual Communication, Fashion, or similar field (mandatory school internship or convention required)
* Intermediate knowledge of Adobe Suite
* Interest in fashion, textile, and visual trends
* Curiosity for new design technologies (AI is a plus, not required)
* Eye for detail and motivation to learn
* Ability to work in a structured way and support multiple projects
* Good communication skills and team spirit
