**Mission Overview:**
 Keystone Solutions is seeking an experienced Azure Data Engineer / Data Scientist for a consultancy mission at a client site. This role is pivotal in delivering strategic data use cases on the Azure Databricks platform as part of the client's 2026 roadmap.
   

  

**Main Mission:**
 As an Azure Data Engineer / Data Scientist, you will bridge data engineering and data science to bring use cases into production. You will build and optimize data pipelines in Databricks, take ownership of existing models, and adapt or improve them as necessary.
   

  

**Key Responsibilities:**
* Design, develop, and optimize data pipelines in Azure Databricks and Azure Data Factory.
* Industrialize models and calculations for reliable production operation.
* Build and support Power BI reports, including data modeling and performance optimization.
* Understand existing models and calculation logic, including anomaly detection models.
* Modify, retrain, validate, and improve models and indicators as business needs evolve.
* Collaborate with Data Engineers, Data Scientists, and business stakeholders to translate needs into deliverables.
* Document data flows, models, and best practices for smooth handover.
