**How will you contribute to the success of DHL?**
==================================================

* Support the execution of data analytics projects in collaboration with the supervisor/project lead.
* Involvement in the analysis of the business processes as well as the derivation of problem areas and analytical requirements.
* Identification and collection of required data as well as data cleansing.
* Develop and implement prototypes for the business users.
* Support the anchoring of data-driven decisions in operating business through the building up of specialist methodical knowledge and knowledge transfer from successfully completed projects.
* Derive analytical requirements, select and execute appropriate mathematical methods, and interpret the results with guidance from supervisor.
* Manage contact with internal and external customers on data analytics topics at the work and specialist level with limited guidance from project lead.
* Relationship management with internal and external expert networks in the data analytics field.

**Do you have what it takes?**

* University degree in Mathematics, Statistics, Physics, Computer Science or related natural sciences / technical degree, or comparable qualification.
* Less than 2 years of Experience
* Deep knowledge of machine learning, statistics and/or mathematical optimization.
* Being familiar with analyzing complex datasets and building mathematical models (theoretical or practical).
* Having programming skills or experience in scripting languages (Python, R or Java).
* Ability to produce creative analytical solutions based on clear and thorough analyses of complex processes and business relationships.
* Comfortable explaining complex analytical and technical content to various audiences.
* Strong team-player, energetic and success-oriented problem solver.
* Independent work ethic, integrity and loyalty, positive “can-do”- attitude."
* Fluent in English, written & spoken. (Other languages are a benefit)
