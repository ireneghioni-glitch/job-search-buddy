**YOUR ROLE**
-------------

In this role you will play a key role in:

* Overseeing and improving supplier performance in terms of **On Time Delivery (OTD)** and **On Conformity Delivery (OCD)** at the group level, implementing structured continuous improvement plans both internally and with suppliers
* Developing supplier maturity through on-site coaching and mentoring, deploying the SPM repository internally and among suppliers, and contributing to supplier selection by providing detailed information and feedback on operational performance
* Ensuring the continuity of deliveries through a robust escalation and crisis management process, overseeing the identification of operational risks for suppliers and managing the associated mitigation plans
* Contributing to industrial capacity planning and stock planning, and supporting the acquisition process through risk and opportunity analyses, review of final supplier choices, and monitoring and controlling the proper delivery of purchase contracts
* Auditing and qualifying suppliers, managing the status of orders, handling special cases, and overseeing the supply of parts and the management of subcontractors in the series production process
* Working in close collaboration with the Industrial Quality team, Purchasing department, and other operational departments across both sites, travelling within Belgium and abroad for approximately **30% of the time** to conduct supplier visits, audits, and performance reviews

**YOUR PROFILE**
----------------

* You hold an engineering degree with a **Quality, Logistics, or Supply Chain orientation** , and bring a minimum of 5 years of experience in a Supplier Quality and/or Purchasing and Order management role, ideally in the defence, aerospace, or a similarly regulated industrial environment
* You have proven experience in supplier performance management, including continuous improvement planning, supplier development, risk management, and crisis management across complex supply chains
* You are professionally proficient in both **French and English** (mandatory, oral and written), with excellent communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively with both internal and external stakeholders across multiple sites and countries
* You demonstrate the ability to work effectively within multidisciplinary teams while remaining autonomous when required, with a structured, rigorous, and solution-oriented approach to managing competing priorities
* You are comfortable operating in an international environment and are willing to travel within Belgium and abroad for approximately **30% of your working time** to conduct supplier visits, audits, and performance improvement activities
