****2 \- Position description****
 The
 **Faculty of Sciences and Bioengineering Sciences** 
 , Department
 **Chemistry** 
 , is looking for a
 **PhD\-student with a doctoral grant** 
 .
   

  

 More concretely your work package,
 **for the preparation of a doctorate** 
 , contains:
   

  

 Your work will focus on the development of new (allosteric) ligands that can bind to membrane\-bound proteins/channels, and on subsequently testing their therapeutic potential.
   

  

  

* You will be responsible for the design and synthesis of bioactive, peptide\-based ligands, including the synthesis of customised amino acids where necessary.
* You will take part in discussions on the design and optimisation of these molecules and contribute to their further development.
* You will be responsible for the in vitro and in vivo evaluation of the therapeutic potential of the synthesised molecules.
* In addition to your research activities, you will also contribute to the supervision of Bachelor’s and Master’s students.


 For this function, our
 **Brussels Humanities, Sciences \& Engineering Campus (Elsene)** 
 will serve as your home base.
   

  

****3 \- Profile****
 What do we expect from you?
   

  

  

* A Master’s degree in science, (bio)medical sciences or an equivalent course of study.
* Experience in experimental research.
* Publications in international peer\-reviewed journals and presentations at international conferences are an advantage.
* A good command of English, both spoken and written, is essential.
* Experience in supervising students.
* A dynamic and flexible approach to work, with the ability to work across two research groups and an eye for detail and accuracy.
* Excellent oral and written presentation and communication skills.
* You have not performed any works in the execution of a mandate as an assistant, paid from operating resources, over a total (cumulated) period of more than 12 months.
* As a (non\-)EEA national, meet the conditions for obtaining a valid permit for VUB and comply with the VUB residence requirements. More info here .
