**Location / Division Specific Information**
 The Chromatography and Mass Spectrometry business develops and manufactures innovative instruments, consumables, and software solutions for customers working in analytical sciences and scientific research. We play a critical role in supporting pharmaceutical and biopharmaceutical organizations through advanced LC\-MS technologies, workflow optimization, and scientific expertise.
   

  

**How Will You Make An Impact**
 The Field Application Specialist (FAS) will provide advanced scientific and technical expertise to customers, sales teams, and internal partners within CMD (Chromatography and Mass Spectrometry Division), supporting LC\-MS and HPLC solutions with a strong focus on Pharma and Biopharma applications.
   

  

 The successful candidate will combine strong technical expertise in LC\-MS and HPLC instrumentation with direct hands\-on experience in pharmaceutical and biopharmaceutical analytical workflows, including method development, troubleshooting, characterization, and operation within regulated laboratory environments. The role also requires a solid understanding of operational processes within structured pharmaceutical and biopharmaceutical organizations, including quality standards, compliance expectations, and cross\-functional collaboration.
   

  

**What Will You Do**
* Deliver advanced product and workflow training to customers virtually, remotely, onsite, or at Thermo Fisher Scientific training centers.
* Support customers in the implementation and optimization of LC\-MS and HPLC solutions
* Act as a trusted scientific advisor for complex analytical challenges in regulated environments.
* Provide scientific and technical support for Pharma customers using LC\-MS and HPLC workflows.
* Support method development, troubleshooting, data interpretation, and workflow optimization.
* Experience in Biopharma applications and biologics characterization is considered a strong plus.
* Deliver scientific presentations, workshops, and customer demonstrations highlighting LC\-MS and HPLC solutions for Pharma and Biopharma applications.
* Collaborate closely with Sales, Marketing, Service, Product Management, and Application teams to support customer success and strategic initiatives.
* Analyze experimental data and provide scientifically sound recommendations.
* Support customer escalations and provide high\-level scientific problem\-solving.
* Communicate effectively with both technical and non\-technical stakeholders.

* Master’s degree or PhD in Chemistry, Biochemistry, Pharmaceutical Sciences, Biotechnology, or a related scientific discipline.

* Ability to collaborate effectively in cross\-functional and international environments.
