INEOS Inovyn is Europe's leading producer of vinyls and in the top three worldwide. With an annual turnover of €5.1 billion, INEOS Inovyn has circa 4,300 employees and manufacturing, sales and marketing operations in 8 countries across Europe.

INEOS Inovyn’s portfolio consists of an extensive range of class-leading products arranged across General Purpose Vinyls; Specialty Vinyls; Organic Chlorine Derivatives; Chlor Alkali; Hydrogen and Performance Chemicals. INEOS Inovyn's annual commercial production volume is circa 10 million tonnes.

See www.inovyn.com and www.ineos.com for more details.

INEOS Inovyn is looking for a Production/Process Engineer for one of its Production Units at the Jemeppe-sur-Sambre site.

Your responsibilities :

* Assist the Production Unit Manager in the daily operations of this asset.
* Study and carry out projects to improve equipment or manufacturing processes.

Your profile :

* Academic: Civil engineer (chemistry, mechanics or electromechanics).
* Junior profile (0 to 5 years of experience).
* Theoretical and practical knowledge in chemical process engineering and materials science will be a definite asset.
* High sensitivity to process safety and occupational safety in an industrial environment.
* Fluent in French and English.
* The position requires, on the one hand, communication, negotiation and initiative skills, and on the other hand, to have rigor in analysis, to be well organized, methodical and to have a critical mind.
* A guard role is required.
* Live within a 30-minute radius of the Jemeppe-sur-Sambre site.

We offer an attractive salary package.
