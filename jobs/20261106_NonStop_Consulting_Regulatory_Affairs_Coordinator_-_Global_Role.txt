**Regulatory Affairs Coordinator | Global Role**
================================================

**Location:** Office Based, Ghent Area

**Introduction**
----------------

Our client is a global chemical manufacturer with a circular, upcycling-led business model. It is looking for a Regulatory Affairs Coordinator to join a well-resourced Global Regulatory Affairs and Sustainability function. You would manage compliance across several jurisdictions for a portfolio that spans demanding legacy chemistries and next-generation materials. You would work with R&D, commercial and technology teams from the start of a product's life. If you want to move from administering files to advising the business, this role is built for that.

**Why This Opportunity Stands Out**
-----------------------------------

The company runs a global network of manufacturing sites across North America, Europe and Asia and sells into more than 70 countries. That means you would manage compliance across EU REACH, UK REACH and US TSCA at the same time, instead of working only on local European submissions. Few regulatory roles in Belgium offer that cross-border scope. It would take you from regional compliance coordinator to a genuinely international regulatory profile, without relocating.

The portfolio is unusually broad. It includes highly scrutinised legacy aromatic chemistries, which sit among the most closely watched substances in front of the European authorities. It also includes newer materials such as battery-grade products for electric vehicles and resins for food-contact packaging. You would work on complex compliance questions, including authorisation and risk management, instead of routine file upkeep. Mastering this level of regulation is the kind of experience that other chemical sectors recognise and respect. Battery and clean-energy compliance is also one of the fastest-growing niches in the field.

Regulatory sits in a centralised product stewardship model and is treated as a strategic partner, not a back-office service. You would be in the room early, advising on product decisions and shaping commercial direction. The company also takes an active part in Brussels-based industry associations and consortia. You would help set data-quality standards for the wider sector instead of just reading regulations after they are published. That builds a strong professional network at the centre of European chemical policy. And because the company is lean and agile, you would gain that influence without the bureaucracy of a large institution.

The sustainability story is real. The business model is built on turning industrial by-products from other processes into high-value materials, so waste reduction is part of how it makes money. Its sustainability credentials are independently rated at the highest medal levels. Your compliance data and reporting would support verified results, not marketing claims, so you could speak about the company's environmental record with confidence. The site is also outside the main chemical-cluster traffic corridors, so you keep a global career with a noticeably easier commute.

**Key Responsibilities**
------------------------

* Manage regulatory compliance across EU REACH, UK REACH and US TSCA, including registration maintenance, dossier updates and volume tracking
* Act as the product steward, partnering with R&D, commercial and technology teams from early product development through to market
* Assess the regulatory impact of new substances, raw materials, process changes and product launches
* Support compliance for complex, highly scrutinised legacy chemistries, including authorisation, restriction and risk management requirements
* Contribute to regulatory strategy for newer product areas, such as battery materials and food-contact packaging applications
* Prepare, review and update Safety Data Sheets, and make sure CLP classification and labelling stay accurate across markets
* Track legislative developments (REACH, CLP, SVHC, restriction proposals, TSCA updates) and advise internal stakeholders on the implications
* Take part in industry association work and consortia, helping to shape data-quality standards and sector positions
* Respond to customer, supplier and authority requests for regulatory documentation and declarations
* Provide accurate regulatory data to support sustainability reporting and independent ESG assessments
* Maintain regulatory records and product data in internal systems, and support audits and inspections
* Help improve regulatory processes, templates and documentation

**Key Requirements**
--------------------

* Degree in chemistry, life sciences, environmental science, chemical engineering or a related discipline
* Hands-on experience in regulatory affairs or chemical compliance at associate to intermediate level
* Solid working knowledge of EU REACH and CLP; exposure to UK REACH and/or TSCA is a strong advantage
* Experience with SDS authoring and hazard communication
* Familiarity with tools such as IUCLID, REACH-IT or SAP EHS is beneficial
* A proactive, business-minded approach: you are comfortable advising colleagues and holding your ground in technical discussions
* Strong organisational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to manage several priorities at once
* Excellent communication skills for working across technical, commercial and external audiences
* **Mandatory requirement:** Fluent English and Dutch. Other European languages are advantageous
* **Location is not negotiable:** Right to work in Belgium and willingness to work from the hiring location.
