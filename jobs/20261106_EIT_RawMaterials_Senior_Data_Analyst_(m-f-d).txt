**About EIT RawMaterials**

As the world moves towards a greener future, the demand for raw materials—minerals and metals—is set to increase immensely over the next three decades. Europe faces a critical challenge in securing a stable supply of these essential resources. This challenge is where EIT RawMaterials steps in.

EIT RawMaterials is at the forefront of driving innovation and sustainability in the raw materials sector. **As an Innovation Community within the European Institute of Innovation and Technology, we collaborate with over 300 partners across industry, academia, and research institutes.** Together, we forge dynamic, long-term partnerships to develop innovative solutions that meet Europe's growing demand for raw materials

### **Position**

**Role Overview**

We are seeking an experienced and highly analytical **Senior Data Analyst (m/f/d)** to transform complex market and business data into actionable insights that drive strategic decision-making. The successful candidate will lead the design and delivery of advanced analytics solutions, develop impactful visualizations, and partner with stakeholders across the business to provide data-driven recommendations. This role combines deep analytical expertise, business acumen, and strong stakeholder management skills, with a particular focus on commodities, minerals, metals, and critical materials markets.

**Tasks and Responsibilities**

**1. Advanced Analytics, Reporting & Visualization**

* Lead the design, development, and maintenance of interactive dashboards, reports, and analytical solutions using Power BI, Tableau, Qlik, Looker, or similar BI platforms.
* Translate complex commodities market data into clear, compelling visual narratives that support executive and operational decision-making.
* Define and maintain enterprise-wide reporting standards, visualization best practices, and dashboard design guidelines.
* Develop scalable self-service BI solutions that empower business users to independently access and analyze data.
* Continuously optimize reporting solutions, incorporating stakeholder feedback and evolving business requirements.
* Drive adoption of analytics solutions through user engagement, training, and continuous improvement initiatives.

**2. Data Analysis & Insight Generation**

* Perform advanced exploratory and diagnostic analyses across commodity prices, market indices, supply-demand indicators, trade flows, and macroeconomic datasets.
* Identify trends, patterns, correlations, risks, and market opportunities within raw materials, advanced materials, and critical minerals markets.
* Develop meaningful KPIs, scorecards, and performance metrics to monitor market developments and business performance.
* Deliver strategic ad hoc analyses to support leadership decisions, commercial initiatives, market intelligence activities, and corporate reporting.
* Present analytical findings through concise reports, presentations, dashboards, and data storytelling techniques tailored to diverse audiences.
* Provide evidence-based recommendations that support business growth, market positioning, and strategic planning.

**3. Data Management, Modelling & Analytics Enablement**

* Collaborate with data engineers, data scientists, and subject matter experts to ensure reliable, scalable, and efficient data pipelines.
* Design and optimize data models, semantic layers, reporting datasets, and analytical frameworks supporting enterprise analytics.
* Define and implement data quality standards, validation processes, and controls to ensure accuracy, consistency, and trust in analytics outputs.
* Support the integration of new internal and external data sources, including APIs, market intelligence platforms, databases, and third-party feeds.
* Document data definitions, transformation logic, business rules, and analytical methodologies to ensure transparency and knowledge sharing.
* Identify opportunities to automate manual reporting processes and improve analytics efficiency.

**4. Stakeholder Consulting & Business Partnership**

* Act as a trusted analytics advisor to stakeholders across Strategy, Business Development, Procurement, Innovation, Finance, Market Intelligence, and Leadership teams.
* Lead requirements gathering sessions and translate business needs into scalable analytical solutions.
* Influence decision-making by providing objective analysis, insights, and recommendations grounded in data.
* Present complex analytical concepts and findings to senior leadership and non-technical stakeholders in a clear and accessible manner.
* Facilitate workshops, training sessions, and knowledge-sharing initiatives to increase data literacy across the organization.
* Mentor junior analysts and contribute to developing analytical capabilities within the team.
* Drive user adoption and engagement through regular feedback collection and enhancement of analytics products.

**5. Governance, Innovation & Continuous Improvement**

* Champion best practices in data visualization, data storytelling, analytics governance, and reporting standards.
* Ensure compliance with data governance, security, privacy, and regulatory requirements.
* Stay current with emerging technologies, methodologies, and market trends in business intelligence, analytics, and commodities markets.
* Evaluate and pilot innovative analytical approaches, including advanced visualizations, predictive analytics, scenario modelling, and forecasting techniques.
* Contribute to the long-term analytics roadmap and continuously improve the organization's data-driven decision-making capabilities.

### **Qualifications & Experience**

**Education**

* Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Information Systems, Statistics, Economics, Engineering, Mathematics, or a related quantitative discipline.
* Equivalent professional experience in analytics, business intelligence, or data visualization will also be considered.

**Professional Experience**

* **5+ years of experience** in Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, Market Intelligence, Commercial Analytics, or a related analytical role.
* Experience working in consulting, professional services, market intelligence, research, commodities, resources, energy, mining, or related industries is highly desirable.
* Strong experience analyzing commodity markets, pricing data, supply-demand dynamics, market indices, trading data, or risk-related datasets.
* Experience within critical minerals, metals, advanced materials, raw materials, energy, or adjacent sectors is preferred.
