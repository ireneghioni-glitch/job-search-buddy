**Your Impact**
 You'll analyze trends and opportunities across the battery value chain \- including cell manufacturing, cathode and anode materials, and battery technologies.
   

  

 You'll build proprietary market models and decision tools, and collaborate with cell makers, OEMs, suppliers and investors to deliver strategic insights.
   

  

 You'll bring a deep understanding of the battery ecosystem \- from raw materials and cell chemistry to gigafactory operations and market dynamics \- and a passion for translating data into strategic recommendations. You should be comfortable using AI tools and basic coding to enhance workflows and modeling.
   

  

 You will be part of the firm's think tank for innovation and decision support across the battery value chain. We help business leaders and policymakers solve critical challenges to accelerate the global shift to electrification and a low\-carbon economy.
   

  

 Working closely with our global network of battery experts, you'll help shape client strategies with data\-backed perspectives on technologies, market outlooks, value pools, and cost and value\-chain transparency across geographies.
   

  

 You'll join one of our hubs – Munich, Brussels or Amsterdam \- as part of the broader Battery Accelerator Team, with 250\+ members, working closely with internal experts with relevant industry experience and academic expertise.
