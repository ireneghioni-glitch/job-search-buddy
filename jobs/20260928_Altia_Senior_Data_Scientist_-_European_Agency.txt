**🔹Role🔹**
 We are looking for a Senior Data Scientist \& Cloud Engineer specialised in High\-Performance Computing, Cloud Architecture, scientific computing, and large\-scale geospatial and oceanographic data processing, combining advanced Python software engineering with extensive experience in migrating and optimising complex computational workflows.
   

  

**🔹Who We Are🔹**
 Altia is a high\-value digital transformation services company that has undertaken, changed, and reformulated international projects for nearly a thousand clients, leaders in their sectors, who have understood that their businesses can be revolutionized through digitalization. Over its 30 years of experience, it has proven to be a robust company with a future, a capacity for innovation, and adaptability.
   

  

**🔹The project🔹**
 You will contribute to an international scientific and technological project supporting European institutions and agencies, focused on large\-scale scientific computing, geospatial data processing, and the development and optimisation of HPC and Cloud workflows.
   

  

 Scientific \& Geospatial Data Engineering: Develop and manage high\-quality global datasets, integrating heterogeneous geospatial and oceanographic data from sources such as OpenStreetMap, satellite products, and scientific observation networks.
   

  

 HPC \& Scientific Computing: Design, develop, and optimise high\-performance computational workflows for large\-scale scientific applications and numerical modelling.
   

  

 Mesh Generation \& Numerical Modelling: Develop workflows for the generation of global unstructured meshes and support the calibration and optimisation of large\-scale ocean modelling configurations.
   

  

 Cloud \& HPC Migration: Design and execute the migration and transfer of complex HPC workflows between Cloud and European HPC infrastructures, ensuring portability, scalability, and reproducibility.
   

  

 Data Processing \& Validation: Build robust processing and validation frameworks capable of handling very large scientific datasets and supporting the evaluation of numerical models.
   

  

 Software Engineering \& Automation: Develop maintainable Python\-based solutions and CI/CD pipelines, using containerisation to ensure reproducible and reliable scientific computing environments.
   

  

**🔹Stack🔹**
 Programming \& Data Science: Python, scikit\-learn, scientific computing and large\-scale data processing.
   

  

 HPC \& Scientific Computing: HPC, High\-Performance Computing, EuroHPC, numerical modelling and computational optimisation.
   

  

 Cloud Architecture: Microsoft Azure, Cloud infrastructure and distributed computing.
   

  

 Containers: Singularity, Apptainer, containerised scientific workflows and reproducible environments.
   

  

 CI/CD \& Automation: CI/CD pipelines, workflow automation and software delivery practices.
   

  

 Scientific \& Geospatial Technologies: SCHISM, GMSH, OpenStreetMap (OSM), oceanographic and geospatial datasets, satellite L3/L4 products and ARGO data.
   

  

 Data Engineering \& Validation: Large\-scale scientific datasets, model calibration, validation frameworks and heterogeneous data integration.
