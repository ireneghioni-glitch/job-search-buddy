**A Day in the Life**
---------------------

As Analytical and PAD Lab Manager, you lead the Analytical and PAD laboratories, ensuring safe, efficient and high-quality execution in line with business priorities. You are accountable for people leadership, resource allocation, technical capability, laboratory performance and continuous improvement.

  

Responsibilities & accountabilities:

* Managing laboratory requests arising from development projects, customer needs and production issues, from initial evaluation through execution, reporting and closure.
* Prioritizing the laboratory portfolio and allocating people, equipment and pilot-line capacity based on business value, urgency and technical complexity to ensure timely delivery of agreed priorities.
* Collaborating closely with Technology, Manufacturing, Quality, Product Stewardship, Technical Service and Commercial teams to translate business needs into effective technical solutions.
* Ensuring compliance with applicable safety, quality and Operational Excellence requirements across all laboratory and pilot-line activities.
* Establishing, validating and regularly reviewing laboratory methods and techniques to maintain up-to-date technical standards and capabilities.
* Driving continuous improvement, digitalization and automation initiatives to ensure efficient laboratory workflows, optimal equipment utilization, shorter turnaround times and high standards of data quality and productivity.
* Developing and presenting CAPEX and OPEX proposals for laboratory equipment, infrastructure, maintenance and continuous improvement projects.
* Building team capability through coaching, cross-training, knowledge sharing and succession planning.
* Providing analytical and application-development expertise to support MBS innovation, customer, and manufacturing and business projects.

**You Bring This Value**
------------------------

* A master’s degree in chemistry or chemical engineering is required;
  + a PhD in chemistry or chemical engineering is an advantage.
* At least 8–10 years of relevant experience in analytical laboratories, polymer or application development, or technical manufacturing support, including several years of people-leadership experience.
* Strong technical knowledge of analytical techniques, polymers, additives and their applications, as well as extrusion and application-testing methods.
* Continuous-improvement experience;
  + Lean or Six Sigma certification is an advantage.
* Experience with laboratory digitalization, automation or LIMS is an advantage.
* Experience with business-specific software, such as SAP and Sopheon.
* Fluency in English and a good command of Dutch.
