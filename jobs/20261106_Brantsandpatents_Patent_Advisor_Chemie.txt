To support our further growth ambitions, brantsandpatents is looking for **a Patent Advisor or (partially qualified) European Patent Attorney with a background in chemistry, preferable organic chemistry**.

As a Patent Advisor, you will analyze the R&D activities of your clients and advise them on how to protect their innovations through an appropriate IP strategy, which you will then put into practice.

Within the patent department, you will work with other patent advisors, -attorneys and patent paralegals. In this varied role, you will be responsible for mapping (technical) inventions, drafting patent applications, and managing and guiding the patent portfolios of clients.

You will keep an eye on competing innovations and advise on potential conflicts when necessary. You will also proactively propose solutions. You will represent your clients before the Belgian Intellectual Property Office and the European Patent Office.

You’ll work from our office in Ghent.

**You have or are...**

* A master’s degree (MSc) in chemistry, chemical engineering or similar, with a strong preference for organic chemistry. A PhD is a nice to have.
* Strong analytical skills and a results-oriented, quality-driven approach.
* A client-driven mindset.
* Fluent in Dutch with a good knowledge of English.
* Interest in legal principles and patent law.
* The ambition and drive to build a career in IP and to qualify as a patent attorney.

**We offer...**

* An exciting and demanding role in a dynamic office with growth ambitions.
* An attractive salary package plus secondary benefits (such as cafetaria plan, hospitalization insurance, group insurance, CAO 90, meal vouchers, ...).
* A 40-hour workweek with 20 statutory vacation days and an additional 12 compensatory leave days.
* A pleasant and flexible working environment with excellent accessibility.
* A training path towards European Patent Attorney, with guidance from experienced colleagues.

**If you are interested, please send your cover letter and CV to hr@brantsandpatents.com. We’re looking forward to hearing from you!**
