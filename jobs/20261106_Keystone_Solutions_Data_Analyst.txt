**Mission Overview:**

  
  

Join Keystone Solutions as a Data Functional Analyst on a consultancy mission at our client site. You will be part of a multi-disciplinary agile team consisting of data engineers, reporting experts, project managers, data architects, data quality experts, and AI specialists, working on dynamic projects that have a significant impact on society.

  
  

**Role & Responsibilities:**

  
  

* Lead discussions with various Subject Matter Experts (SME) to understand their data-related needs.
* Identify, assess, and understand business processes, operational, and analytics data flows (incoming and outgoing).
* Translate business needs into data and technical requirements.
* Design and validate data models in collaboration with Data Architects.
* Establish functional design for developments in collaboration with the Data Governance team, BI Technical Leads, and SDO (ICT) teams.
* Ensure GDPR and security compliance in contact with the DPO.
* Oversee testing of developed data products.
* Inform, train, and encourage adoption among business users.
  

  

**Your Technical Skills:**

  
  

* Master's degree in Computer Science or equivalent experience.
* Minimum 5+ years of professional experience in data functional analysis.
* Concrete experience in data warehouse modeling (Kimball approach).
* Experience in designing and analyzing data flows.
* Prior experience as a data engineer is a plus.
* Minimum 3+ years of concrete experience with MS on-premise stack (SQL Server, SSIS, SSAS, PowerBI).
* Experience with Azure Databricks is a plus.

* Excellent communication skills (speaking, reading, writing), mastering both national languages professionally (French & Dutch: B2+ minimum).
