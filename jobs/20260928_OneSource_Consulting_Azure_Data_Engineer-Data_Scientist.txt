**JOB DESCRIPTION:** 



* As an Azure Data Engineer / Data Scientist, you will bridge data engineering and data science to bring our use cases into production before the end of the year.
* You will build and optimise data pipelines in Databricks, take ownership of the models and indicator calculations already developed by the team, and adapt or improve them where needed.



  





**ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:** 



* Design, develop and optimise data pipelines in Azure Databricks and Azure Data Factory to feed the use cases.
* Industrialise models and calculations so they run reliably in production (scheduling, monitoring, data quality checks).
* Build and support Power BI reports, including data modelling, DAX measures, report design, performance optimisation and alignment with business reporting needs.
* Quickly understand the existing models and calculation logic, including anomaly detection models (statistical methods and Isolation Forest) and indicator calculations.
* Modify, retrain, validate and improve these models and indicators when business needs or data evolve.
* Collaborate with Data Engineers, Data Scientist and business stakeholders to translate needs into concrete deliverables.
* Document data flows, models, calculation logic and best practices to ensure a smooth handover at the end of the assignment.



  





**REQUIRED SKILLS:** 



* Proficiency with Azure Databricks and Azure services: Data Factory, Azure Storage, Key Vault, Azure DevOps.
* Strong experience with Spark/PySpark and Delta Lake / Lakehouse architectures.
* Strong skills in SQL and Python (pandas, scikit\-learn).
* Strong experience building Power BI reports, including semantic/data modelling, DAX, visual design, performance optimisation and deployment best practices.
* Knowledge of statistics and machine learning, in particular anomaly detection techniques (e.g. statistical thresholds, Isolation Forest).
* Ability to read, understand and take over existing code and models developed by others.
* Adhere to best practices
