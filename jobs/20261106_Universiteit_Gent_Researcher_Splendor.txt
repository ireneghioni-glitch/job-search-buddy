**ABOUT GHENT UNIVERSITY**

  

Ghent University is a world of its own. Employing more than 15,000 people, it is actively involved in education and research, management and administration, as well as technical and social service provision on a daily basis. It is one of the largest, most exciting employers in the area and offers great career opportunities. With its 11 faculties and more than 80 departments offering state-of-the-art study programmes grounded in research in a wide range of academic fields, Ghent University is a logical choice for its staff and students.

**YOUR TASKS**

  

* At least 70% of your assignment will be spent on academic researchin the framework of chemistry and engineering: experimental determination of kinetics performances including advanced characterization techniques, kinetic modeling, reactor design and process analysis. Particular attention will be paid to lignin valorization at supercritical operating conditions within the SPLENDOR project.
* Up to 10% of your time will be spent of scientific communication among project partners and project management.
* Up to 10% of your time will be spent on teaching assignments.
* Up to 10% of your time will be spent on coaching of students at the master and PhD level.

  

**WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR**

  

* You hold a Master’s degree in Chemical Engineering or equivalent by experience;
* Previous experience with intrinsic kinetic data acquisition and advanced modeling, beyond the research work performed to acquire the master degree is required.
* You are an expert in set-ups for intrinsic kinetics data acquisition, more specifically in the area of heterogeneously catalyzed reactions. Experience in analyzing the interplay of different operating variables on the chemical kinetics is available.
* Proven track record of technical writing through scientific publications in international peer-reviewed academic journals that are widely disseminated.
* Presentations at international research symposia.
* Excellent communication skills in English – both oral and written.
* International mindset and mobility, in view of the promotion of the interaction and collaboration between the project partners.
* Experience and proven interest in mentoring activities is considered as an advantage.
* Living in Belgium or ready to stay in Belgium during the research
